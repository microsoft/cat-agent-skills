# Section templates

Reusable skeletons for the pack. Angle-bracket fields are filled from workshop.json; the quality gate fails
on any that remain. Use them when the docx or pptx skill writes the files instead of the bundled scripts,
so both paths produce the same structure.

> <format> and hands-on: <n> activities take <minutes> of the <length>. Everything taught comes from
> <source titles>.
