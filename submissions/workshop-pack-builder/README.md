# Workshop Pack Builder

Give it your reference material on any topic and get back a workshop you can run. The builder reads your decks, documents, PDFs, web pages and notes, designs a session around them and produces every file a facilitator needs. Every fact in the pack traces back to one of your sources.

## What you get

- **Agenda:** a full agenda and a one-page landscape view, with durations that add up to the session length.
- **Slide deck:** module slides, exercise slides and source references, styled from your PowerPoint template when you supply one.
- **Facilitator guide:** talk track, setup, timings, debrief questions and a fallback for every module.
- **Participant workbook:** exercises, worksheets and space for notes.
- **Optional extras:** a pre-read, a feedback survey, a follow-up email draft and an HTML page.

Every document opens with an executive summary.

## How it designs the session

- Three to five learning objectives, each backed by your sources.
- A five-part arc for each module: why it matters, teach, do it, best practices, take away.
- At least one hands-on or discussion activity in every module, and at least 40% of the session hands-on or discussion.
- Standard duration labels only (5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 45 or 60 minutes), checked by a bundled timing script.

## Example prompts

- "Build a 90-minute workshop from these three files."
- "Turn this deck and this article into a half-day training with exercises."
- "Create a virtual workshop on change management for new managers. Here are my notes."
- "Make a facilitator guide, handouts and a survey for this session, using our brand template."

## What you need

- Python 3 with python-pptx, python-docx and pypdf.
- Optional: LibreOffice, pdftoppm and Pillow for the visual slide check.
- Optional: web access for URLs and Microsoft 365 file tools for OneDrive or SharePoint files.
- Optional: the pptx, docx or a SharePoint page skill. Each has a bundled fallback, so the skill works on its own.

## Good to know

- **Grounded in your sources.** The builder never invents statistics, quotes, prices or links. Anything your sources don't cover is listed as a gap, not filled in.
- **Quality gate.** A bundled check confirms timing math, objective coverage, complete exercises, cited sources and the writing rules before the pack is called ready.
- **Fictional practice data.** Exercise examples are labeled illustrative and never use real customers or people.
- **Drafts only.** It never sends invites, emails participants or schedules the session.
