# Default minutes structure, presentation conventions, citation protocol and Word path

Loaded on demand by `draft-minutes-and-resolutions`. Nothing here overrides the guardrails in
SKILL.md. A supplied house format model overrides the structure and presentation defaults below —
but never the evidence, placeholder or draft-marking rules.

## Default structure

1. **Header** — entity, governing body, meeting date, start and end time, location or platform, and
   the marking **Draft minutes — not yet approved**.
2. **Attendance** — Present; In attendance; Apologies; and any attendance change (late arrival,
   early departure) against the agenda item it affected.
3. **Quorum** — the recorded quorum statement, or "Not addressed in the transcript". Never a
   conclusion that quorum was met.
4. **Declarations of interest** — declarations and recusals as recorded, or "None recorded".
5. **Previous minutes / matters arising** — only where discussed.
6. **Agenda items** — numbered; for each, the discussion and then the outcome, labelled
   `Resolution`, `Decision`, `Noted` or `Deferred`. Use numbered sub-paragraphs for distinct threads.
7. **Resolutions** — full text, resolution number, recorded vote, and the drafted/not-verbatim label
   where the wording was not adopted verbatim.
8. **Action items** — always the table `Action | Owner | Due date | Agenda item`, using
   `[Owner to confirm]` and `[Due date to confirm]` where the transcript is silent.
9. **Any other business**, then **Date of next meeting**.
10. **Coverage & Assumptions appendix** — sources and read status; inventory counts (agenda items,
    resolutions, decisions, actions); evidence anchors (speaker and timestamp per outcome and
    action); `Needs review` items; defaults applied; and every placeholder used.

## Presentation conventions

- **Bold every heading and sub-heading**, including agenda headings and each sub-paragraph lead-in.
- **Number sub-paragraphs within an item** (5.1, 5.2, 5.3).
- **Use real lists where the content is a list**: attendance, apologies, declarations, actions,
  resolutions and appendix inventories.
- **Do not bullet the narrative of a debate.** Keep its qualifications and conditions in prose, so a
  conditional approval reads as conditional.
- Where the creation path cannot apply a Word list, use textual numbering rather than typed bullet
  characters; never leave headings unbolded.

## Chat citation protocol

When **In chat** was selected, keep the minutes in house style and place citations in the
Coverage & Assumptions appendix:

1. Finish the draft and the evidence map, then plan the complete citation set before writing the
   reply. Do not assemble citations incrementally.
2. For each source location supporting a substantive outcome or action, quote its exact verbatim
   text — the most specific paragraph or list item, including a paragraph inside a table cell.
3. Attribute each quote to the exact filename plus speaker and timestamp, or clause, section or
   page. Never invent a locator.
4. Put each attribution beside its human-readable source locator in the appendix, reusing one
   attribution where a single location supports several entries.

Every supplied file is citable this way. Preserve retrieved and connector sources through verified
links, names, speakers, timestamps and other reader-facing locators. Never expose workspace paths or
internal tool names.

## Word creation path

Skip this path entirely when the user selected **In chat**; in that case give the complete minutes
and resolutions in the reply and never create or claim a file.

1. Finish the minutes, resolutions, actions, evidence map, appendix and placeholders first.
2. Write `working/content-brief.md` as the authoritative handoff: the complete content, structure,
   exact facts and reader-facing evidence locators, with no internal identifiers.
3. Hand that brief to Cowork's built-in Word document creator as content authority; it owns placement and
   formatting.
4. Creation formats only. It must not re-read sources, reclassify outcomes or add governance facts.
5. Confirm the file passes the creator's validation and visual QA, and confirm it exists from the creator's report before naming it.
6. Keep the delivery reply to counts, `Needs review` items, defaults applied, placeholders and the
   exact filename. Do not paste the document contents into chat.

Cowork's built-in document creators narrate their own work — do not add a task step for generic creation tools.
