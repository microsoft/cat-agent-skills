# Worked example — weekly matter summary

Loaded on demand by `summarize-legal-matters`.

The user asks for a weekly summary of three matters: Redgrave (`RDG-2231`), the
Halloway lease, and a regulatory query. They name the Litigation Support shared
mailbox and the Redgrave Teams channel as in scope, pick *partner* depth, and attach
a counsel opinion circulated on 19 August. "This week" resolves to 21–28 August 2026,
and each matter is searched separately across email, Teams, calendar and the two
named shared sources — and no others.

A settlement figure appears in an email, a forward, a Teams chat and a meeting
invitation: that is **one** development, reported once under Redgrave's Material
developments with all four links in the entry. A covering email says only "revised
deed attached, as discussed"; the skill opens the attachment, finds the payment term
moved from 14 to 30 days, and reports that, citing the email of 21 August **and** the
Draft Settlement Deed at clause 4.2. A scanned exhibit with no text layer is listed by
name as unread. The 19 August counsel opinion is read for context and cited, but not
listed as this week's development. A partner writing "I'd be minded to accept subject
to the client" is **not** a decision: it appears labelled `Proposed - subject to client
instruction`, with the absent instruction under Open questions. A directions deadline
that fell on 26 August with no evidence of compliance appears as **`OVERDUE`**, and the
summary says the sources do not show whether it was met — it does not assert a breach.
