# SOP quality rules

A complete SOP should include:
1. Purpose
2. Scope and exclusions
3. Definitions
4. Roles and responsibilities
5. Inputs and prerequisites
6. Procedure steps
7. Decision and exception handling
8. Controls and evidence
9. Systems and records
10. Metrics and service levels
11. Risks
12. Revision history
13. Open questions

Use direct, executable language. Do not hide missing information behind generic wording.
