# Sales

A Microsoft-built Cowork plugin with nineteen skills that help sellers prepare
for customer engagements, understand deal movement, position value, and build
customer and leadership presentations.

## What's included

| Area | Skills |
| --- | --- |
| Account and meeting preparation | Create Account Brief; Meeting Prep; Stakeholder and Decision Map; Post-Meeting Follow-Up |
| Deal and pipeline insight | Customer Engagement and Deal Risk Insights; Deal Catch-up; Pipeline Prioritizer; Create Pipeline Review |
| Positioning and value | Business Case Composer; Competitive Strategy; Prepare Objection Responses |
| Customer presentations | Create Sales Pitch; Create Proposal Response; Create Customer Business Review; Create Client Portfolio Review |
| Reporting and slides | Sales Progress Update; Create Executive Sales Summary; Visualize Sales Data; Reimagine This Slide |

## Before you start

Name the customer, deals, or portfolio in scope and the period you care about.
Provide the relevant meeting notes, CRM exports, workbooks, decks, or other
authorized sources, and say who the output is for. Share only material you are
permitted to use for this task.

## Try it

> Prep me for Thursday's call with Northstar. What do I need to learn, prove,
> and ask, and what is the best next move?

> Which of my open deals need attention this week? Explain the evidence behind
> each risk and who owns the blocker.

## Limitations

The skills work from the evidence available and mark what is missing or
unconfirmed rather than guessing. They do not send customer communications,
schedule meetings, or change CRM records without your explicit request. Review
drafts, figures, and competitive claims before sharing them with customers or
leadership.

## Install

1. Download the plugin ZIP from this page.
2. Upload it through **M365 admin center > Manage apps > Upload custom app**,
   or sideload it for testing with the Microsoft 365 Agents Toolkit CLI:
   `atk install --file-path <zip> --scope Personal`.
3. Open **Cowork > Sources & Skills > Plugins** and enable it from **Discover**.

See [Get started with Cowork](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/get-started-with-cowork)
and [Build plugins for Copilot Cowork](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/cowork/cowork-plugin-development).
