# Sales

A Microsoft-built Cowork plugin with nineteen skills that help sellers prepare
for customer engagements, understand deal movement, position value, and build
customer and leadership presentations.

## What's included

| Skill | Workflow |
| --- | --- |
| Business Case Composer | Build a customer-specific business case connecting the problem, cost of inaction, solution, investment, and supported value |
| Competitive Strategy | Position against competitors with supported differentiators, honest tradeoffs, and objection responses |
| Create Account Brief | Build an internal account brief deck for a named customer or prospect |
| Create Client Portfolio Review | Prepare a client-facing investment portfolio review for financial and wealth advisors |
| Create Customer Business Review | Build a recurring customer business review or QBR of goals, results, adoption, and commitments |
| Create Executive Sales Summary | Summarize sales results, forecast, risks, and decisions for senior leadership |
| Create Pipeline Review | Build an internal opportunity and forecast review for sales management |
| Create Proposal Response | Build a proposal or bid presentation that responds to buyer requirements or an RFP |
| Create Sales Pitch | Build a customer pitch deck for first meetings, product introductions, and industry pitches |
| Customer Engagement and Deal Risk Insights | Explain deal momentum, evidence-backed risks, blockers, and their owners |
| Deal Catch-up | Summarize what changed across a scoped set of deals since a baseline |
| Meeting Prep | Prepare for one upcoming customer conversation with an evidence-backed next move |
| Pipeline Prioritizer | Rank open opportunities into a worklist of what to work next and why |
| Post-Meeting Follow-Up | Capture decisions and commitments from a completed meeting and draft the follow-up |
| Prepare Objection Responses | Prepare evidence-backed responses to a customer's likely objections |
| Reimagine This Slide | Build three alternative versions of one existing slide |
| Sales Progress Update | Create or refresh a sales progress update for a manager or team |
| Stakeholder and Decision Map | Map the buying group, likely roles, and the customer's decision process |
| Visualize Sales Data | Turn sales, pipeline, and financial data into executive-ready slides |

## Before you start

Name the customer, deals, or portfolio in scope and the period you care about.
Provide the relevant meeting notes, CRM exports, workbooks, decks, or other
authorized sources, and say who the output is for. Share only material you are
permitted to use for this task.

## Try it

> Prep me for Thursday's call with Northstar. What do I need to learn, prove,
> and ask, and what is the best next move?

> Which of my open deals need attention this week? Explain the evidence behind
> each risk and who owns the blocker.

## Limitations

The skills work from the evidence available and mark what is missing or
unconfirmed rather than guessing. They do not send customer communications,
schedule meetings, or change CRM records without your explicit request. Review
drafts, figures, and competitive claims before sharing them with customers or
leadership.

## Install

1. Download the plugin ZIP from this page.
2. Upload it through **M365 admin center > Manage apps > Upload custom app**,
   or sideload it for testing with the Microsoft 365 Agents Toolkit CLI:
   `atk install --file-path <zip> --scope Personal`.
3. Open **Cowork > Sources & Skills > Plugins** and enable it from **Discover**.

See [Get started with Cowork](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/get-started-with-cowork)
and [Build plugins for Copilot Cowork](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/cowork/cowork-plugin-development).
