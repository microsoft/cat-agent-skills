# Create Client Portfolio Review

## Establish the client's question

Create a client-facing investment portfolio discussion from authoritative reports,
the supplied mandate, client concerns and any advisor proposals. Establish what
the client wants to understand or decide, the relevant accounts, reporting dates
and delivery audience. Ask for missing information that changes the analysis;
complete conclusions that remain independently supported. This review does not
select investments or authorize transactions.

## Work within the host and report permissions

Host/user/brand/template authority takes precedence over this Skill's defaults.
Use the host's production and QA workflow. For brand work, follow its brand
workflow, read the applicable brandkit and build from the governing template.
Reuse a verified applicable template when available; fonts and colors alone are
not template use. Report a missing required template. Ordinary source styling
yields to authorized migration, while explicit report locks and disclosures remain.

Establish permitted transformations and distribution before changing or sharing
reports. Historical approval does not automatically cover rewritten calculations
or redesigned material; apply actual reapproval requirements without inventing
a universal compliance gate. Preserve locked wording, labels and geometry. Use
an allowed explanation layer around protected reports rather than claiming they
were rebuilt. A screenshot is fixed evidence, not an editable dataset.

Distinguish live viewing, client copies and internal preparation. Delivery to
the requester does not itself authorize client distribution. Branding cannot
expand these permissions. Attribute reported evidence, author calculations and
advisor proposals separately; required disclosures retain their prescribed wording
and location.

Read required evidence completely before using it; recover truncation and record
gaps in host work records. Pause only dependent work when essential evidence is
unavailable. Read [Financial interpretation](references/data.md) before explaining
or retaining financial values, returns, benchmark comparisons, allocations or
funding calculations, including evidence on protected pages.

## Explain what changed and why it matters

Give each slide a distinct purpose and make its key answer the visual focus.
Choose a form that reveals the relationship; use a table when exact lookup is
the main task. Omit repeated content and empty containers that add no meaning.
Keep material qualifications beside their claims.

Answer the client's question before presenting supporting detail, including
material uncertainty. Make performance gaps, allocation versus policy ranges and
cash headroom directly comparable where relevant. Use compatible bases and
earlier/later states when explaining requested changes. Give performance, flows,
allocation and liquidity distinct roles: investment
result, money movements, exposure and conditional funding feasibility. Use the
roles relevant to the client's question.

Keep exact holdings or movements in one permitted lookup location and preserve
any required report order. Titles and causal or comparative wording must use the
same basis as their evidence.

## Address the complete mandate

Relate the supported stance to each material supplied growth or income objective,
loss tolerance, horizon, liquidity need, policy range and prohibition. Explain
what supports maintaining or adjusting the position, the tradeoff and what
remains unconfirmed. Cash sufficiency answers a liquidity question; range
compliance answers a policy question. Neither establishes the complete mandate,
and benchmark deviation alone is not a mandate breach.

If risk evidence is missing, explain supported exposure or liquidity implications
and name the unavailable risk judgment. Do not invent a drawdown estimate or
declare all analysis impossible. Do not assume goals or circumstances are unchanged
without evidence.

Complete supported after-payment cash, asset-class proportions, policy feasibility
and conditional alternatives even without an advisor recommendation. Distinguish
internal funding from new external money and calculated minima from extra buffers.
Explain each alternative's conditions without choosing a security or funding
holding, inventing a NAV, sensitivity range or forecast, or treating an illustration
as a valuation.

## Close on the remaining decision

Separate reported evidence, calculations, planning assumptions, advisor proposals
and client authorization. Explain a supplied proposal's rationale, tradeoffs and
actual approval state. A historical balance does not guarantee future availability:
retain relevant intervening-flow, balance, settlement and authority rechecks.
Arithmetic feasibility is not transaction authorization. Close with the supported
stance or understanding, tradeoffs and unresolved conditions, rather than another
data summary. Do not invent actions or dates.

## Scenario checks for host QA

Use the host's QA on final previews to check that the key answer is visually
prominent and that labels, dates, codes and qualifications remain readable
without awkward wraps or collisions. Check unused placeholders, unintended font
changes and consistency with the applicable template. Preserve intentional layout
variation and protected pages; report unresolved defects.

- Does the opening answer the client's question, with requested changes directly
  readable on compatible earlier/later evidence?
- Does the maintain/change rationale address the supplied mandate beyond cash
  or range compliance, with missing risk judgments identified?
- Are flows, performance, benchmark bases, calculation status and funding
  alternatives correct, without invented investment choices or valuations?
- Are report protections, disclosure wording, transformation authority and
  viewing/distribution rights preserved? Check disclosure text separately from fit.
- Do shortened conclusions retain feasibility, settlement and authorization
  conditions without turning analysis into a trade instruction?
