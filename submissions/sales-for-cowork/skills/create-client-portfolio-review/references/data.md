# Financial interpretation

Read before using financial values, returns, benchmarks, allocations or funding
calculations, including retained protected evidence. Keep account scope, currency,
cutoff and source attached to each measure.

## Values, flows and performance

Where the source defines a reconciliation, verify opening value plus net external
flows plus investment change equals closing value. Separate external flows from
transfers within the consolidated portfolio; a funding transfer may be an internal
allocation. Preserve recorded purposes rather than explaining a historical
withdrawal with a later spending request.

A lower balance need not mean negative performance. Distinguish totals from
included components: fee detail already contained in investment change is not
another subtraction. Do not invent residuals to force reconciliation. Historical
reconciliation does not prove future cash availability.

Use the supplied return method, including linking or compounding where applicable.
Retain period, annualized/cumulative status, fee basis, currency and scope. Value
change is not investment return when flows or methods differ. Do not invent TWR,
MWR, annualization or fees when their definitions are missing.

## Benchmarks and explanations

Use a supplied applicable benchmark and identify its components/weights, method,
period, currency and fee basis. A generic name cannot define a blended benchmark.
Compare compatible bases or explain their mismatch. Essential definitions remain
with the comparison; detailed methodology may use permitted references.

Check comparative and causal claims against the stated basis. Positive returns
can trail a benchmark; recovery needs a comparison point. Attribution requires
supplied components and a method. Contributions, holding returns and weights are
different measures. Without attribution, give qualified context rather than
inventing performance drivers. Keep historical results separate from forecasts
or authorized planning assumptions.

## Status, precision and funding

Identify reported values, author calculations and proposals locally. Final and
provisional measures retain their own dates and status; dependent calculations
inherit input uncertainty without making unrelated finalized measures provisional.
Unknown values are not zero.

Distinguish percentage points, basis points and relative percentage change.
Retain calculation precision and round display values consistently; rounded
labels cannot support a more precise derived gap. Round a minimum funding amount
upward when needed to satisfy its executable unit. Label an extra buffer separately
instead of calling a larger feasible amount the minimum.

Allocation percentages require a defined whole without overlapping components.
Policy comparisons use compatible scope, date and basis. In funding alternatives,
state whether money comes from an internal sale or outside the portfolio; these
can affect different parts of the denominator. Calculate supported post-payment
cash and class proportions without assuming which holding funds the payment.
Cash feasibility may still conflict with another mandate condition.

Preserve signs and component relationships in the exhibit. A movement diagram
must reconcile; do not exaggerate small flows or make an included fee look like
an additional step. Keep source values and author transformations distinguishable
even when they share a provider citation.
