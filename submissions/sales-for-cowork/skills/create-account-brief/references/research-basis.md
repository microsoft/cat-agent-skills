# Why this account brief structure

Original synthesis of inspected public artifacts, accessed October 14, 2026.
These are templates or practitioner guidance, not verified completed customer
briefs or evidence of improved sales results. Their facts are not current account
research. No source decks/PDFs or proprietary diagrams are redistributed here.

| Source | Inspected form and locator | Useful pattern; limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |

Together these motivate a customer-first decision sequence: business/priorities,
people and current position, plausible value, then engagement actions. The
approximately ten-main-slide default, source appendix, evidence-status labels,
and delivery checks are this skill's design choices, not a
claim that the sources all prescribe one exact outline.

Account plans, sales pitches, and QBRs are adjacent, not interchangeable. A brief
prepares the team to understand and engage one account; it does not fabricate a
long-range account strategy or persuade the customer with internal tactics.
