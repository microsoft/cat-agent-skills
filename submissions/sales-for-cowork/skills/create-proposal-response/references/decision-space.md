# Allocate response tables
Optional within the host's planning and chosen design. For a dense response, commercial or conditions register, consider the audience question, primary fields, complete required-ID set, chosen body type, usable width/height, field widths, row capacity, continuations and homes for moved fields. Budget from the longest actual cells at readable type, not equal columns or the source shape.

The response view keeps ID, answer/state and decisive evidence/limit adjacent. Put a constant field in the scope/header; mark exceptions instead of repeating it in every row. Move only background fields. Retain all mandated answers and repetitions in readable native continuations with repeated IDs/headers, indexed from the primary view. Notes may hold provenance, not replace required answers or material conditions.

Split rows or complementary fields when cells need compressed type/spacing. Capacity follows content, not the default slide budget; do not force a register onto one page. A summary/diagram explains fit rather than repeating the complete inventory.

Status uses words plus contrast. Emphasize quoted totals inside the table with currency, period and mandatory/optional scope. Give the evaluator's decision/clarification, consequence, authority and date a primary area; routine follow-ups stay secondary. Context imagery yields space to exact lookup.

Compare the final render's actual allocation to the plan. Verify every required ID/field at its intended home and that viewers can locate status, material limit, total, owner and date without dense cross-cell tracing. If reading still fails, reallocate and rerender; a written plan or complete field count is not success.
