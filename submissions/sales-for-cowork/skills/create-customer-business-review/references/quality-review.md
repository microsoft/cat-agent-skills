# Narrow quality review
For quality/service scope.

## threshold-context
Bind result to authorized threshold/operator/period/unit/direction and equivalent population. Complaint rates need denominators; first response differs from closure. Label closed-case averages; retain open cases separately. Compare actual/threshold directly; no target means result/question, not attainment. Improvement is not compliance; unknown is not failure.

## exception-action
Prioritize supplied severity or comparable deviation. Bind issue ID, evidence, cause status, action and owner/date. Keep hypotheses/proposals labeled; update is not completion. Aggregate-only data permits investigation, not invented cases. No exception permits monitoring.

## delivery-confirmation
Separate dated vendor delivery and customer confirmation under one issue/action ID. Formal acceptance requires evidence; missing confirmation is not rejection. Preserve cutoff/completion dates. Separate retrospective results from next proposals; do not invent approval, ROI or expansion.
