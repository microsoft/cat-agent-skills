# Composition patterns

Choose one primary pattern for each page's question. Retain the relationships
below, not exact coordinates or example claims. Content, count, palette and
imagery can adapt; a conflicting restriction calls for a compatible pattern.
Full-page compositions are textual guidance, not slides to import.

## photo-statement

**Job:** default cover, promise, transition or recognizable relationship close.
**Hierarchy:** an edge-to-edge contextual photograph with subject on the right;
native dark overlay on the left only when provider rules permit; small label, short accent rule and large
low-left statement. Leave the subject exposed and edge metadata quiet.
**Adapt:** re-use an opening image at close with a brief thank you or invitation
to continue. Crop only when the image provider permits it; Ghostrider results
are slot-ready and must not be cropped, masked or post-processed. Request quiet
contrast-safe image composition when overlay permission is absent.
Keep words editable. An inset image or centered logo does not retain this pattern.

## ring-evidence

**Job:** explain a measured partition with one supporting claim.
**Hierarchy:** substantial ring on the right, total at its center, associated
category/count/share labels; concise assertion and one emphasized share on the
left. The ring dominates, not a row of metric cards.
**Adapt:** `allocation.partition-ring` supplies native quantitative geometry.
Categories must be disjoint and exhaustive, summing to the whole; distinguish
count from share and keep scope and denominator visible.
A categorical ecosystem ring cannot acquire percentages without actual data.

## section-wayfinding

**Job:** orient the audience at a real transition in the argument.
**Hierarchy:** recognizable Agenda label/number, one prominent section question
and a brief bridge from the preceding conclusion to the following content.
**Adapt:** use open space, a supported keyword or permitted contextual imagery;
keep native editable text. Match Agenda wording/order without repeating its whole
list. Omit standalone dividers for an incompatible short/custom brief; ordinary
body-slide titles and repeated empty section pages are not navigation.

## two-anchors

**Job:** make a specific gap visible through two grounded facts or keywords.
**Hierarchy:** short large thesis on the left; two open evidence anchors stacked
vertically on the right. Each starts with oversized value/keyword, subordinate
unit and a compact definition. Generous vertical separation; no enclosing cards.
**Adapt:** use categorical anchors when numbers are unavailable. Omit quote
glyphs unless real testimony exists. Use two distinct facts, not the same result
repeated. A three-number row is a different pattern.

## boundary-hero

**Job:** connect a product proposition to three useful levers.
**Hierarchy:** dark upper/light lower fields; assertion upper left; substantial
product/context image upper right crossing the color boundary; three open
icon/label/caption groups share a baseline in the lower field.
**Adapt:** use an honest generated product-workspace concept when real UI is
unavailable. Preserve image dominance and boundary crossing; no fabricated
screenshot or three framed prose cards. Peer levers do not imply a sequence.

## milestone-spine

**Job:** show meaningful chronology.
**Hierarchy:** one continuous thin rail through round, icon-bearing nodes; short
dates above and meaningful event captions below, open whitespace and coherent
color progression. `timeline.icon-phases` supplies the component.
**Adapt:** use 3–6 actual milestones, reflow captions before shrinking; spacing
is categorical, not elapsed time. A no-icon variant needs a compatible explicit
adaptation. Do not flatten nodes into rectangles or detach them from the spine.

## arrow-stations

**Job:** explain input, action and output with retained human decisions.
**Hierarchy:** three native stations on one baseline, connected left-to-right by
two solid right arrows; a short label and editable description at each station.
`process.three-stations` supplies the component.
**Adapt:** group detailed actions under the three phases without losing approvals,
evidence checks or release. Arrows are not chevrons or a narrowing funnel;
equal visual spacing does not imply equal durations.
Reserve a full-width lane when labels need it. Reflow the whole component:
short station headings, separate readable descriptions, consistent clearance,
and connectors meeting the intended nodes. Replace inherited overlapping
placeholder boxes. Do not imply that independent channels are mandatory stages,
or fit by deleting defining icons or shrinking text below the design floors.

## criteria-matrix

**Job:** compare alternatives fairly on the same definitions.
**Hierarchy:** native table; wide colored criterion stub, shared option columns,
aligned intersections and quiet alternating row bands. Circular option markers
above the columns are identifiers, not ratings.
**Adapt:** use `comparison.criteria-matrix`; select a symmetric subset of relevant
rows across all options. Highlight the focal offer, keep alternatives neutral,
and preserve literal unknowns. Do not substitute dots or invented scores.

## photo-number-split

**Job:** put bounded evidence beside relevant context.
**Hierarchy:** open evidence field with a short assertion, dominant number
and adjacent definition beside a substantial photograph. Allocate their widths
by evidence length and image aspect, not a fixed half-and-half split.
**Adapt:** two paired observations can support one range highlight with readable
task values below. Keep study date, sample, method and first-draft scope visible.
Context photography is not a headshot/testimonial or a picture of the actual case.
An image cannot certify an outcome.

## open-commitment

**Job:** make one next decision actionable.
**Hierarchy:** large title, short accent rule and premise above three spacious
open columns; accent action labels and dark scope text, no tier cards.
**Adapt:** use actions, responsibilities or decision stages under one proposed
commitment. Retain permissions and terms; do not invent prices, QR destinations,
trial rights or contacts.

## title-image

**Job:** editorial introduction without a full-image requirement.
**Hierarchy:** oversized left title reaches toward one substantial inset image
on the right of a dark field; quiet edge metadata and open lower-left space.
**Adapt:** deliberate title/image tension must not obscure either. This is not
full-bleed and cannot satisfy that request.

## story-proof-rail

**Job:** relate a compact story to two or three verified observations.
**Hierarchy:** dark story/photo field left, light continuous proof rail right
with vertically stacked evidence frames; numbers dominate their captions.
**Adapt:** use one consistent frame treatment and authorized or contextual
imagery, retaining local definitions. Keep the rail subordinate to the story,
not an equal-card dashboard.
