# Vendor Contract Risk Review

A first pass over a vendor contract or SOW before it goes to legal: the
clauses that commonly cause disputes later (auto-renewal traps, one-sided
liability caps, vague termination terms, unclear IP ownership), flagged by
risk level with plain-language explanations.

## What it checks

Auto-renewal and opt-out notice, termination rights and fees, liability caps
and their carve-outs, indemnification balance, data and IP ownership, SLA
remedies, pricing escalation, governing law and arbitration, and assignment
rights. The full list and what each one means is in the skill's instructions.

## What it explicitly is not

Legal advice. Every response makes clear this is preparation for a human
legal or procurement review, not a substitute for one. It doesn't draft
replacement contract language either; it describes what a more favorable term
typically looks like and leaves the actual drafting to counsel.

## Why it doesn't over-flag

A lot of automated contract review tools flag everything, which trains users
to ignore the output. This skill is explicit about distinguishing "unusual or
one-sided" from "definitely a problem," and closes with a short prioritized
list instead of a wall of findings.
