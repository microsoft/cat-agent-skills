# Function and industry examples

**Illustrative examples only.** These are the advisor's own examples to help a leader picture each pattern in their area. They are not Microsoft claims, product commitments or customer results. Label them that way in every output. Adapt them to the user's actual initiatives; never present them as things the organization already does.

Pattern abbreviations: EAE Employee AI enablement; BEE Business expert empowerment; WIT Workplace and IT services; CBP Core business process transformation; EXT External engagement; AIF AI-first capabilities.

## Business functions

### Sales
- EAE: account research briefs and meeting prep for each seller
- BEE: a pricing and contract-terms advisor grounded in the approved deal desk playbook
- CBP: quote-to-order orchestration with approval limits and exception routing
- EXT: a website agent that qualifies inbound leads and books meetings, with AI disclosure
- AIF: continuous territory and pipeline sensing that proposes coverage changes

### Customer Service
- EAE: case summaries and reply drafts for service representatives
- BEE: a knowledge advisor for complex product and warranty policy, owned by a named expert
- WIT: internal service desk for representatives (access, schedule swaps, tooling requests)
- CBP: returns and refunds handled end to end within set value limits
- EXT: tier-1 customer self-service for order status and troubleshooting, with escalation to a person

### IT
- EAE: script and documentation drafting for engineers
- BEE: architecture standards advisor grounded in approved reference architectures
- WIT: password resets, access provisioning and device troubleshooting run end to end
- CBP: change management triage and release coordination across systems
- AIF: anomaly detection and automated first response across monitoring signals

### HR
- EAE: job description and interview guide drafting for hiring managers
- BEE: a benefits and policy advisor owned by the HR policy lead
- WIT: leave requests, onboarding workflows and policy questions handled as a service
- CBP: hire-to-onboard orchestration across HR, IT and facilities systems

Note: never use agents in this skill's outputs to rank, score or evaluate individual employees.

### Finance
- EAE: variance commentary drafts for analysts
- BEE: accounting policy advisor grounded in the controller's manual
- WIT: expense validation and invoice processing with exception routing
- CBP: financial close reconciliation and accrual preparation within thresholds

### Communications
- EAE: first drafts of internal announcements and speaker notes
- BEE: brand and style advisor grounded in the approved brand book
- CBP: content calendar and approval workflow orchestration
- EXT: a press and stakeholder inquiry agent that answers from approved statements only

### Sustainability
- EAE: summarizing supplier sustainability reports for analysts
- BEE: reporting-standards advisor grounded in the organization's approved methodology
- CBP: emissions data collection and validation across business units
- AIF: continuous sensing of energy use with recommended actions within limits

### Accessibility
- EAE: alt text and accessible document checks for content authors
- BEE: an accessibility standards advisor grounded in the organization's guidelines
- WIT: accommodation request intake and routing as an internal service
- EXT: accessible self-service for customers, tested with assistive technology users

## Industries

### Financial services
- BEE: product and regulatory policy advisor for branch staff
- CBP: loan application document checks and routing within credit policy limits
- EXT: customer servicing agent for balance, card and dispute status, with disclosure and identity checks

### Manufacturing
- EAE: shift handover summaries for supervisors
- BEE: quality procedure advisor on the plant floor
- CBP: inspection triage and defect routing
- AIF: predictive maintenance loops that schedule work within set limits

### Retail
- EAE: store manager briefings
- WIT: store staff HR and IT requests as a service
- EXT: order status and returns agent for shoppers
- AIF: continuous pricing or replenishment optimization within guardrails

### Public sector
- BEE: caseworker policy advisor grounded in current regulations
- WIT: internal procurement and HR services
- EXT: resident inquiry agent with clear disclosure and handoff to staff

### Healthcare and life sciences
- EAE: administrative documentation drafting for non-clinical staff
- BEE: policy and procedure advisor for operations teams
- WIT: scheduling and facilities requests as internal services
- Note: treat clinical use as Tier 3 at minimum, require human decision makers, and point to the organization's clinical governance.
