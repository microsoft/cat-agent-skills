# Insight Synthesis Rules (cited as insight-rules.md #<n>; engine constants mirror sections)
## 1. Retrieval
1.1 Studies rank by market + topic + recency match to the question. Relevance is stated per study; nothing is cited that was not retrieved.
## 2. Trends
2.1 Trend deltas compare like-for-like: same metric definition, same market. Cross-definition comparisons are flagged, not blended.
## 3. Contradictions
3.1 Two studies moving the same metric in opposite directions are BOTH presented with dates and methods - contradictory evidence is flagged, never smoothed into a midpoint.
## 4. Provenance & rights (Govern)
4.1 Every cited study carries a rights check: license scope (internal/external), geography, expiry. A restricted or expired study is unusable FOR THAT USE regardless of how strong the stat is.
4.2 The brief's use-case (internal deck vs external/board/press) is declared up front and gates every citation.
4.3 The provenance record ships with the brief - who can reuse it, where, until when.
## 5. Scope
5.1 Retrieval, comparison, citation only: no demand forecasts, no claim approval, no causal attribution, no analysis over large tabular files (known capability boundary).
