# Marketing & Sales Brainstorm Lab

For when you need real variety, not one polished idea dressed up three ways.
This skill researches the actual market and competitive landscape first, then
runs a structured divergence step before narrowing down, so the ideas you get
back are genuinely different from each other, not near-duplicates.

## How it works

Research, then diverge, then converge. It looks up competitor positioning,
recent campaigns, and audience context before generating anything (or asks
you for what you already know, if it has no web access). Then it runs one of
four structured ideation techniques (`references/ideation-techniques.md`) to
force genuine variety instead of defaulting to the first idea. Finally it
narrows to the strongest few, with the real tradeoff named for each: reach vs.
cost, risk vs. novelty, safe vs. attention-grabbing.

## How it fits with other skills in this gallery

This is where ideas get generated. Once you've picked a direction, hand off to
the skills built for execution: [`b2b-outreach-suite`](../b2b-outreach-suite)
for outreach copy, [`linkedin-content-system`](../linkedin-content-system) or
[`pov-line-generator`](../pov-line-generator) for content and positioning
lines, and [`competitive-battlecard-builder`](../competitive-battlecard-builder)
for a structured comparison. This skill won't reproduce their output formats;
it points to them instead.

## What it won't do

Present a made-up statistic or invented competitor detail as fact, or hand
you three versions of the same idea with different words. Anything not
sourced gets labeled as an assumption to verify.
