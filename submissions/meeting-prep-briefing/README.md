# Meeting Prep Briefing

A short pre-read for the minutes before a meeting: who you're meeting, where
things stand, what's still open, and two or three things worth having a
position on. Built from what's actually available, not padded to look
thorough.

## How it's different from meeting-analyzer

This gallery already has [`meeting-analyzer`](../meeting-analyzer), which
produces a deep intelligence report *after* a meeting from a transcript. This
skill is the other side: a tight briefing *before* a meeting happens, built
from prior notes, related documents, and (for external meetings) public
context on the other party. Different moment, different job.

## Why it stays short

A briefing nobody reads before walking into a meeting isn't useful. This
skill is built to summarize tightly and offer more detail on request, rather
than defaulting to a long document. It's also explicit when there's nothing
to report, since a confident-sounding briefing built on gaps is worse than an
honest "no prior history found."
