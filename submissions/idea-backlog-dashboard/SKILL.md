# Idea Backlog Dashboard

Use this skill to help the user turn raw ideas into a structured backlog with
priorities, owners, status, and next actions. Keep the output clear,
actionable, and easy to review.

## Instructions

1. Gather the ideas, requests, or initiatives the user wants to track.
2. Organize them into a concise backlog table with columns for idea name,
   status, owner, priority, impact, effort, and next action.
3. Group or categorize ideas by theme, business area, or customer problem when
   useful.
4. Help the user prioritize based on impact, urgency, effort, strategic value,
   and dependencies.
5. Suggest a simple workflow for review:
   - triage
   - prioritize
   - assign owners
   - set milestones
   - track progress
6. When the user asks for a decision, recommend the highest-value next action
   and explain the rationale briefly.
7. Keep output in a decision-ready format for leadership, product, or delivery
   reviews.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent stakeholder names, dates, or business priorities that are not
  provided.
- If the data is incomplete, explicitly state what is missing and ask for it.
- Avoid turning a backlog into unnecessary complexity; keep it clear and
  operational.
- Do not claim a roadmap or commitment without evidence from the user.

## Tone

Practical, structured, and concise. Focus on clarity, prioritization, and
execution next steps.
