# Default Prep Pack Outline

**Workflow boundary:** a named institutional investment-client mandate review, or its consultant/trustee review, anchored to IPS/guidelines and relationship commitments. Not the manager's own board/forecast review, a generic business pre-read, or standalone stakeholder Q&A. A template cannot expand this job.

Structure for `institutional-client-review-prep`. A house template in
`Cowork Templates/Asset Management/institutional-client-review-prep/` may replace this layout, subject to the guardrails below.

A house template governs **layout and section structure only**. It never removes citations, gap markers, source tiers, approval states, information-barrier rules, no-send restrictions, or the output footer — those apply to every output regardless of template. Output length never waives attribution or the footer; a one-line answer, single table cell, inline reply, or short excerpt still carries both. If a save, export, or write fails, report what was produced, where it was and was not written, and what remains incomplete.

**Two separate documents.** The briefing is internal; the deck is client-facing. Internal
observations must not leak into the deck.

---

# Part A — Internal briefing (`.docx`)

## 1. Meeting facts

Client / consultant · date and time · attendees with organization and role · format · duration.

## 2. Relationship snapshot

Mandate(s), inception date, and the date of the prior review. Factual only — no assessment of
how the relationship is going.

## 3. Open items

The section people actually read.

| # | Item | Raised | Source | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Provide updated fee schedule | 2026-05-14 | prior review notes | OPEN |
| 2 | ESG reporting frequency question | 2026-06-02 | email, client CIO | UNCLEAR |

`OPEN` / `CLOSED` / `UNCLEAR`. **Never mark `CLOSED` without record evidence.** `UNCLEAR` means
the record does not show resolution — which is exactly what the owner needs to check before
walking in.

## 4. Recent interaction summary

Notable correspondence in the search window, most recent first, each citing the source and
date. Include unanswered threads explicitly.

## 5. IPS / guidelines summary

Objective, constraints, permitted ranges, reporting obligations — as documented.

**No compliance assessment.** Whether the portfolio sits inside its ranges requires holdings
and the system of record.

## 6. Discussion topics

Drawn from open items and recent correspondence. Questions the client is likely to raise, based
on what they have actually asked before — not on what clients generally ask.

## 7. Prep gaps

What could not be found and where you looked.

---

**Audience-separation preflight.** Before finalizing the client artifact, compare it against every source item marked internal/reviewer-only. Exclude the private content, its paraphrases, preparation/ownership checks, and metadata revealing what was excluded. Do not write a client-facing sentence such as "an internal note about <topic> was excluded" — that still discloses the topic. Only the separate internal briefing may discuss those notes. Keep independently supported client commitments visible in client-appropriate wording; do not suppress a legitimate client open item merely because an internal preparation note concerns the same general subject. Apply this check to slide text, speaker/source notes, appendices and the client footer; a generic statement that a separate internal briefing exists must reveal no private subject.

# Part B — Client review deck skeleton (`.pptx`)

Follow the **prior review deck's structure** where one exists — it reflects what this client
expects. This outline is the fallback.

1. **Cover** — client name, period, date
2. **Agenda**
3. **Firm and team update** — approved boilerplate only
4. **Market backdrop** — approved house view, or cited public macro
5. **Performance** — `[PERFORMANCE — populate from system of record]`
6. **Attribution** — `[ATTRIBUTION — populate from system of record]`
7. **Portfolio positioning** — `[HOLDINGS — populate from system of record]`
8. **Guideline compliance** — `[COMPLIANCE — populate from system of record]`
9. **Strategy commentary** — from the approved commentary archive
10. **Open items and next steps** — the `OPEN` items from Part A, client-appropriate wording
11. **Disclosures** — the firm's standing block

Slides 5–6 remain value-less until current field-appropriate system-of-record data is available **and** the presentation gate permits this actual external client deck. Then, and only then, use the eligible values verbatim; a current export alone never unblocks a page. Slides 7–8 need their own appropriate current holdings/compliance system-of-record records; a performance connector cannot supply that authority. Assess intended use separately for the internal briefing and the client deck, preserve permitted historical references only in separate internal reviewer records, and never carry numbers forward from the prior deck.

Footer on every deck: `MARKETING MATERIAL — COMPLIANCE REVIEW REQUIRED`.


## Performance fields

Apply the shared performance-presentation gate in `house-rules.md` to each actual artifact/field. For externally intended performance, missing/inapplicable policy, unresolved intended use, incompatible data, missing required net performance or unmet substantive requirements leaves value-less blocked placeholders; current source data alone does not unblock it. Continue the rest of the draft and record actual intended use, policy and gate outcome in the footer. Preserve confirmed-internal-only current-source figures with basis/internal labels, and historical PRIOR values confined to clearly separate internal reviewer records; neither may be copied into proposed/external fields without passing the external gate. Existing answer states, refresh flags and internal/client separation remain unchanged.
