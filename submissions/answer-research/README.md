# Answer Research

A Microsoft-built Agent Skill for Cowork that investigates a bounded support,
policy, eligibility, or troubleshooting question.

## When to use it

Use it when you need to establish what the evidence supports, reconcile
sources, or identify missing information. If the answer is already established
and you only need wording for a customer, use Draft Customer Reply instead.

## Before you start

Provide the question, relevant customer or case facts, and the scope of the
investigation. Include applicable policies, guidance, or other authorized
sources, with dates or versions where available.

## Try it

> Research this support question using the attached guidance. Explain which
> sources apply, where they disagree, and what remains unknown.

## What you get

A concise summary and one editable internal research brief containing the
answer, source-backed reasoning, applicability checks, and evidence gaps.

## Review and control

This is an individual Cowork skill. A missing or inaccessible source is not
proof that something is absent or ineligible. Review the findings before
using them in a customer response or decision; research does not send a reply
or change the case.
