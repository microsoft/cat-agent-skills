# Draft Customer Reply

A Microsoft-built Agent Skill for Cowork that drafts a reviewable support
response grounded in known facts, applicable policy, and customer needs.

## When to use it

Use it to draft a ticket response, complaint reply, status update, or a revision
to tone, length, or language. Use Answer Research first when the underlying
answer still needs investigation.

## Before you start

Provide the customer's question, known case facts, applicable policy, and any
communication constraints. Specify the audience, channel, and desired tone.
If you want a native email or CRM draft, state that explicitly.

## Try it

> Draft a customer reply using these verified case facts and policy excerpts.
> Keep it concise and identify anything I need to check before sending.

## What you get

A concise chat summary and a saved editable customer-only reply draft.
When you request an email or CRM draft, that native draft is the primary
review artifact rather than an extra Word file. Saving depends on the
available authorized tools and destination.

## Review and control

This is an individual Cowork skill. Review facts, promises, recipients, and
policy applicability before sending. A saved draft is not a delivered
message; the skill does not send automatically.
