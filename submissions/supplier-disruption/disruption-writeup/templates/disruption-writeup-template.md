# Supplier disruption draft structure reference — not a fillable template

Only scripts/disruption_writeup.py renders the fixed Markdown (.md) report after
recomputing and checking impact, mitigation, notifications and next-best action.
No audience-selection flag, alternate voice, model-filled sections or DOCX/PDF
conversion is implemented. Explain unsupported requests and ask whether the existing
Markdown draft is acceptable.

The runtime layout includes open items/escalations, impact summary/table,
recommended mitigation, unsent owners to review, supplier follow-up draft,
internal escalation brief, provenance and checked packet appendix.
Missing response-by remains an explicitly incomplete draft only when requested.

Preserve the 1.4.1 quantity boundary in this 1.4.2 follow-on: buyer/planner review
must separately determine feasible PO-level quantity and timing. Aggregate
original-need-by demand risk is not a quantity to purchase, substitute or expedite.
No PO issuance, hold or cancellation is instructed.

No messages, supplier commitments, purchases, freight spend, ERP or schedule
changes occur in this plugin, even after human approval. Notifications and
follow-ups remain unsent drafts; authorized humans act outside this plugin.
