# Design Standards - Working Excerpts (grounding for standards-check)
- ASME Y14.5: tolerances are part of the definition of fit; relaxing a tolerance on a mating
  feature changes the fit class and requires mating-part stackup verification.
- Internal design rule DR-12: press-fit bores keep bilateral tolerance <= 0.10 mm total band.
- Internal design rule DR-31: fatigue-critical surfaces (drawing-note designated) may not have
  finish or tolerance relaxed without stress-analysis sign-off.
- Documentation: every characteristic change re-baselines the referencing inspection plan.
