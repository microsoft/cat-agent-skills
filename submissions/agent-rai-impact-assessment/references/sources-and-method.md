## Runtime rules

- Verify current product support, feature behavior, and legal or regulatory claims from authoritative sources at assessment time. Framework documents cannot establish that a particular product configuration provides a control.
- For legal obligations, obtain the jurisdiction and use context, consult current authoritative sources, and hand the open questions to the responsible reviewer by Q-ID. This assessment is not a substitute for a required legal instrument.
