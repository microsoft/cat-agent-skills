# Associate Assist Rules (cited as assist-rules.md #<n>; engine constants mirror sections)
## 1. Answer discipline
1.1 Every answer cites the clause, attribute or promotion line it came from. No citation, no answer - escalate instead.
1.2 A question outside the policy library's scope is out of scope: draft an escalation, never improvise policy.
1.3 Clause ranking: an exclusion clause outranks the general clause only when one of its distinguishing topics (e.g. marketplace, third-party, amazon, ebay) is present in the question. A generic match never promotes an exclusion.
1.4 Returns: the standard return clause (RET-2.1) resolves eligible only when `question.facts.receipt` is in_hand AND the question names an item in a category the clause lists AND `question.facts.purchase_date` is inside that category's window (fashion 30 days, electronics 15 days; `windows_days` in the policy library). Receipt not_in_hand -> the receiptless clause if the policy library has one, otherwise a shift-lead escalation; receipt unclear or a category the clause does not cover -> shift-lead escalation (all below the confidence floor). Purchase date outside the window -> not eligible. Purchase date null -> "eligible only if within <n> days of purchase - confirm the receipt date" below the confidence floor. "Eligible" is never given without a receipt in hand and a purchase date inside the window.
## 2. Price and promotion
2.1 Promotion eligibility = promo window contains today AND store's cluster is in the promo's cluster list AND SKU on the promo list. All three, deterministically. Missing date or cluster -> not checked, never assumed; a missing cluster means the store master export is missing or has no row for the store - ask the user for it.
2.2 Price-match applies only to eligible competitor types in the policy; marketplace / third-party sellers are excluded when the clause says so. The associate never overrides price - eligible or not, execution is a manager action.
2.3 A raincheck (PRC-3.3) is offered only when `question.facts.stock_at_this_store` is out_of_stock AND the promo is live for the asked date at the store's cluster (promo_check eligible). Item in_stock at this store -> no raincheck (the promo applies at the shelf). Promo ended or store not participating -> promo not eligible, no policy alternative; a non-participating store also gets a manager escalation for any advert dispute. Stock unclear or promo status unknown -> escalate and ask (confidence below the floor), never offer.
## 3. Comparison
3.1 Product comparisons state attribute differences from the PIM extract only; missing attributes (absent or null) are shown as MISSING, not guessed, and reported to the PIM steward. Every attribute the associate names is added to `question.compare_attributes` before the engine runs, so a named attribute the PIM lacks appears as a MISSING row (never filled in) with the PIM-steward note.
## 4. Confidence floor
4.1 Resolution confidence < 0.75 (a question spanning more than one policy family, an exclusion matched without a distinguishing topic, or a clause requirement from #1.4 / #2.3 that could not be confirmed, including an unclear receipt, unclear stock or a null purchase date - confidence 0.6) -> escalate to shift lead with the draft attached.
## 5. Data discipline
5.1 The payload carries store, role and department context only - never customer names, contact details or payment-card data.
5.3 The floor answer states the determination, its conditions, the promo result and the escalation from the payload only. It never repeats the associate's asides, hearsay or prior-practice claims from the question text (what a former manager or other staff used to do). When promo results exist, the answer carries the three-leg promo table and the alternative line copied from promo-check's output (`promo-check.md`); answer-draft never builds them itself and runs promo-check first when that output is missing. With no comparison rows the answer has no comparison section, heading included.
5.2 Facts are captured at intake, not inferred by the engine: question-intake records `question.facts` (receipt in_hand / not_in_hand / unclear; stock_at_this_store in_stock / out_of_stock / unclear; purchase_date or null) with the verbatim evidence. in_hand only when the customer clearly has the receipt with them now; out_of_stock when the associate says this store has none, including a plain "is out of stock" / "is sold out" with no other place named, and nothing says units exist here (sold out online or at another store says nothing about this store; any units here mean in_stock); a purchase date only from an explicit date or an exact relative phrase. Anything else is unclear / null. A payload without facts is rejected by the engine.
