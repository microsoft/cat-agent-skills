# Audit Readiness Rules (cited as audit-rules.md #<n>; engine constants mirror sections)
## 1. Evidence recency
1.1 Evidence has a max age per clause (management review 12 months, internal audit 12 months, calibration per schedule, training on assignment). Older evidence is STALE - a full folder is not a current folder.
## 2. Coverage
2.1 Internal-audit evidence must cover EVERY in-scope process area in the cycle; one missing area is a coverage gap on 9.2 regardless of how many audits were done elsewhere.
## 3. Severity
3.1 Gaps on clauses marked major (9.2, 9.3, 8.7, 7.2 competence for active roles) are MAJOR findings-in-waiting. 3.2 Everything else is minor.
## 4. Readiness verdict
4.1 Any major gap -> NOT READY (named gaps, owners, actions). 4.2 Only minors -> CONDITIONALLY READY. 4.3 No gaps -> READY.
## 5. No paper-over rule
A gap is closed by producing the evidence, never by rewording the pack. The pack lists gaps verbatim from the engine.
