Paraphrased operational summaries so contract citations resolve inside the plugin; the

## 8.7 Control of nonconforming outputs
- 8.7.1 Identify and control nonconforming outputs to prevent unintended use or delivery:
  correction, segregation/containment, informing the customer, authorization under concession.
- 8.7.2 Retain documented information describing the nonconformity, actions taken,
  concessions obtained, and the authority deciding the action. (The NCR is this record.)

## 8.4.3 / supplier information
- Certificates of conformance are supplier declarations; they do not substitute for
  verification activities the organization has determined necessary. When verification
  results contradict a certificate, the verification results govern and supplier
  corrective action is evaluated (feeds disposition-rules.md #4.3).

## AS9102 note
First Article Inspection applies only when the lot is a first article (new part, revision
change, process change, or production lapse). Standard incoming lots cite ISO 9001 8.7.
