# Case Preparation Brief

A Microsoft-built Agent Skill for Cowork that prepares a decision-ready brief
on one support issue before the next interaction or action.

## When to use it

Use it for a case handoff, incident review, complaint briefing, or an
assessment of what remains open. It can work from one substantive description
or interaction, not only a complete case record. Use Case Triage to rank
several issues instead.

## Before you start

Identify the case, incident, ticket, or complaint. Supply the available
history, customer need, commitments, and relevant policy or troubleshooting
evidence. Specify the next interaction or decision you are preparing for.

## Try it

> Prepare an internal handoff brief for this case. Explain what happened,
> which commitments remain open, and the recommended next step.

## What you get

A concise summary and one editable internal case-preparation brief covering
status, material history, open commitments, alternatives, and evidence gaps.
It is not a customer-facing reply by default.

## Review and control

This is an individual Cowork skill. Proposed fixes and follow-ups are not
reported as completed without evidence. Preparing a brief does not close,
reassign, or otherwise change the case.
