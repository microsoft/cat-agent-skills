# Service

A Microsoft-built Cowork plugin with five skills that help service teams
prioritize, investigate, respond to, and learn from customer issues.

## What's included

| Skill | Workflow |
| --- | --- |
| Answer Research | Research a source-backed answer to a support, policy, eligibility, or troubleshooting question |
| Case Preparation Brief | Reconcile what has happened on one case and what to do next |
| Case Triage | Rank several issues by impact, deadlines, commitments, and evidenced urgency |
| Draft Customer Reply | Draft a reviewable customer-facing reply grounded in the known facts and policy |
| Knowledge Harvest | Turn a closed case or verified workaround into reusable knowledge |

## Before you start

Provide the cases, tickets, or customer messages in scope and the facts
available for each. Include applicable policies, guidance, priority criteria,
or other authorized sources, with dates or versions where available.

## Try it

> Rank these support issues for today's work. Explain the priority drivers,
> allow ties, and flag information that could change the order.

> Research this support question using the attached guidance, then draft a
> reply to the customer once the answer is established.

## Limitations

The skills mark missing or unconfirmed information rather than guessing. A
missing or inaccessible source is not proof that something is absent or
ineligible. Replies are drafts for your review and are not sent automatically,
and the skills do not reassign, close, or publish anything on their own.

## Install

1. Download the plugin ZIP from this page.
2. Upload it through **M365 admin center > Manage apps > Upload custom app**,
   or sideload it for testing with the Microsoft 365 Agents Toolkit CLI:
   `atk install --file-path <zip> --scope Personal`.
3. Open **Cowork > Sources & Skills > Plugins** and enable it from **Discover**.

See [Get started with Cowork](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/get-started-with-cowork)
and [Build plugins for Copilot Cowork](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/cowork/cowork-plugin-development).
