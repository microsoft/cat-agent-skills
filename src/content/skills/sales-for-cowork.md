The Sales for Cowork helps sales professionals turn customer understanding into action, using people knowledge, communications, and business context to build relationships and drive business outcomes.

Copilot Cowork users can delegate sales-related work using natural language and receive actionable results based on available information from connected systems or materials they provide. Whether preparing for customer engagements, understanding customer priorities, drafting follow-ups, or identifying next actions, the app helps sellers spend less time gathering context and more time engaging customers.

Designed for sales professionals across industries, the app extends Copilot Cowork with sales-focused intelligence that helps sellers prepare, engage, and follow through.

> **Cowork plugin.** This is a Microsoft 365 Copilot **Cowork** app package (a `.zip` bundling the skills and connectors below). It installs on Cowork only.

## Skills in this plugin

- **meeting-prep** — Prepares a seller for one upcoming customer conversation such as what to learn, say, prove, and ask, with an evidence-backed next move and a private editable brief. Use when asked to "prep my customer call", "brief me before my sales meeting", "prepare for this buyer demo", "plan my renewal conversation", or "build talking points for this opportunity meeting". Do NOT use for past-meeting recaps, broad account plans, pipeline audits, seller coaching, standalone pitch decks, or automatic outreach.
- **business-case-composer** — Builds a customer-specific business-case draft connecting the problem, cost of inaction, proposed solution, investment, and supported value. Use when a customer-specific deal or initiative needs a decision-ready qualitative or quantitative business case, such as "draft a business case", "build the value case", or "justify this initiative". DO NOT use for generic product pitches or price and discount quotes.
- **competitive-strategy** — Builds customer-specific competitive positioning with supported differentiators, honest tradeoffs, objection responses, and claims to avoid. Use when a customer use case needs supported competitive positioning, comparison, objection handling, or discovery hypotheses, such as "position against this competitor", "answer this competitive objection", "compare options for this customer", "which competitive claims are safe to use", "build a battlecard for this deal", or "how do we beat this competitor here". DO NOT use for business-case or ROI modeling, a full meeting brief, generic product pitches, or unsupported competitor claims.
- **customer-engagement-and-deal-risk-insights** — Explains customer engagement and deal momentum, identifying evidence-backed risks, blockers, their owners, and meaningful seller actions. Use this skill when a seller asks "which deals need attention", "what changed with a customer", "what is blocking progress", whether a deal has lost momentum, who owns the blocker, or what they can do next. Support both portfolio questions and questions about one deal. Do not use to predict win or close probability, infer deal risk from low activity alone, claim authoritative pipeline risk without live CRM, or take unapproved external action.
- **deal-catchup** — Summarizes what changed and why across a scoped deal set, separating genuine customer movement from record housekeeping. Use to report what changed and why across a scoped set of deals since a stated baseline, such as "which deals slipped", "what moved on my top deals", or "what changed while I was out". Needs a defined scope and current deal evidence; a baseline improves the comparison, but a current-state result is available without one. DO NOT use for ranking what to work on next or a single deal's full standing review.
- **pipeline-prioritizer** — Ranks a scoped set of open opportunities into an evidence-backed worklist of what to work next and why, with ties and unknowns where needed. Use when a scoped visible opportunity set needs a ranked or tied worklist explaining what deserves attention next and why, such as "which deals should I work today", "rank my open opportunities", or "prioritize this deal list". DO NOT use for change digests, meeting prep, or full team pipeline reviews.
- **post-meeting-follow-up** — Turns one completed sales meeting into an accurate summary of decisions and commitments, proposed next actions, and an optional customer follow-up draft. Use when one completed sales meeting or call needs accurate capture, proposed durable actions, and safe customer follow-up, such as "summarize this completed call", "capture meeting commitments", or "draft the meeting follow-up". DO NOT use for upcoming meeting prep, account-wide activity summaries, or lead re-engagement campaigns.
- **sales-progress-update** — Creates or refreshes a sales progress update for a manager or team about work done, deal movement, pending outcomes, supported forecast position, and help needed. Use when asked to "create my weekly sales update", "refresh my sales progress deck", "report my forecast position", "where do I stand against my quarter forecast", or "catch my manager up on committed deals". Do NOT use for non-sales project updates, a since-date deal change digest, momentum or blocker analysis, meeting prep, or outreach drafting.
- **stakeholder-and-decision-map** — Mines customer interactions to identify stakeholders, infer their likely roles and engagement, map the decision process, and highlight who or what may be missing. Use when asked to "map a deal's buying group", identify influential or missing stakeholders, "assess who has gone quiet", or "explain how the customer will decide". Do not use for phased engagement plans, contact sequences, choosing or drafting outreach to the single next contact, seller or team performance reviews, or contacting, scheduling, or changing records.
- **create-customer-business-review** — Create a recurring customer-facing business review or QBR of agreed goals, results, adoption, value evidence, risks, commitments and next-period priorities. Use for "prepare our customer QBR" or "review outcomes and commitments with the customer". Do NOT use for internal account-health assessment or account planning, pipeline management, a new sales pitch, or edits to an existing deck.
- **create-executive-sales-summary** — Create a sales results and forecast summary for senior leadership, highlighting expected outcomes, material risks and decisions requiring executive support. Use for "summarize sales results for our leaders" or "forecast summary for the VP". Do NOT use for deal-by-deal pipeline management, customer-facing business reviews, proactive pitches, or edits to an existing deck.
- **create-pipeline-review** — Create an internal opportunity and forecast review for recurring sales management, covering stage, amount, movement, blockers, risks and next actions. Use for "review this week's pipeline" or "which deals need manager intervention?" Do NOT use for senior-leadership results or forecast summaries, customer adoption or account-health reviews, broad team updates, or edits to an existing deck.
- **create-proposal-response** — Create a buyer-specific proposal or bid presentation responding to explicit requirements or an RFP, with coverage, evidence, delivery, commercials and limits. Use for "respond to the buyer's requirements" or "prepare our RFP proposal deck". Do NOT use for a proactive customer product or solution pitch, investor pitches, customer business reviews, or edits to an existing deck.
- **create-sales-pitch** — Required for general-purpose customer Pitch Decks: cold first meetings, warm-lead product introductions, industry pitches, and internal enablement. Use for "pitch our product to a new buyer" or "introduce our solution". Do NOT use for formal RFPs or buyer-requirement proposals, investor/fundraising pitches, QBRs, account plans, pipeline reports, or edits.
- **create-account-brief** — Use this skill first for creating a source-grounded internal account brief for a named customer or prospect - what matters to the customer, who matters in the decision, where we stand, which opportunities to validate, and what to do next. Saves an editable PowerPoint deck. Use for requests such as "create an account brief for Northstar", "brief our sales team on this customer before Thursday's call", "make an account briefing deck from these notes and filings", or "prepare a prospect brief", even when the word sales is not used. Do NOT use for creating login or user accounts, customer-facing sales pitches, QBRs or adoption reviews, annual account plans with quotas or pipeline forecasts, summarizing or fact-checking an existing deck, drawing a reporting hierarchy alone, briefs wanted as a Word, PDF, or Markdown file, or research answered in chat with no brief file requested. For matching tasks, load this skill before any generic PowerPoint/presentation skill.
- **prepare-objection-responses** — Prepare a seller to handle a specific customer's likely objections. For each objection: the customer's wording, discovery questions to ask live, a short evidence-backed response, the proof behind it or the evidence still missing, and a confirming next step. Delivered as removable slides appended to the end of the current deck, or kept in chat. Use when the user asks to "prep me for this customer's objections", "how do I answer their pushback on price", "build objection handling for this deal", "what will the buyer push back on and how should I respond", "objection responses for the concerns they raised on the call", or "talk tracks for handling procurement's objections". Do NOT use to stress-test, score or find weaknesses in a presentation's own argument, to play devil's advocate, for general Q&A rehearsal, to write speaker notes or a talk track for the deck itself, or to build or redesign a pitch.
- **visualize-sales-data** — turn sales, customer, usage, value, pipeline, and financial data from crm systems, excel workbooks, and other connected sources into one or more executive-ready powerpoint slides. use to visualize data, create charts or scorecards, add data-driven insights to a deck, update existing slides with current metrics, compare customers or periods, explain business performance, or build an executive sales narrative from quantitative data.
- **create-client-portfolio-review** — Prepare a client-facing investment portfolio review using current positions, performance versus benchmark, attribution, and proposed portfolio changes, with required compliance-approved language. Primarily for financial and wealth advisors. Not for general B2B account reviews, portfolios of customer accounts, or sales pipeline reviews.
- **reimagine-this-slide** — Builds three genuinely different, non-destructive alternatives of one existing PowerPoint slide so the user can pick a real slide instead of describing one; each adds meaning (comparison, benchmark, insight, better visual), not just reformatting. Use when working on one slide and asking for other ways to communicate its point: "reimagine this slide", "give me three slide ideas", "how else could I show this?". Also for a selected text, chart, or image ("reimagine this chart"). Do NOT use for copy editing, pure chart restyling, multi-slide deck creation, or simple moves/resizes.

## Install

1. Download the plugin package (the `.zip` on this page).
2. Upload it to your tenant via **M365 admin center › Manage apps › Upload custom app**, or sideload it for testing with the [Microsoft 365 Agents Toolkit CLI](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/platform/toolkit/microsoft-365-agents-toolkit-cli) (`atk install --file-path <zip> --scope Personal`).
3. Open **Cowork › Sources & Skills › Plugins** and enable it from the **Discover** section.

See [Build plugins for Copilot Cowork](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/cowork/cowork-plugin-development) for details.
