Analyst work for banking, research and private equity. Helps with company profiles, comparable company analysis, merger models, CIM teardowns, document diligence, diligence tracking, earnings notes, coverage meeting prep and debriefs, pitch deck skeletons and investment committee memos. Every output states its source and date, marks assumptions rather than inventing them, stays analytical support rather than advice, and never sends or shares anything without your confirmation.

> **Cowork plugin.** This is a Microsoft 365 Copilot **Cowork** app package (a `.zip` bundling the skills and connectors below). It installs on Cowork only.

## Skills in this plugin

- **cim-teardown** — Tears down a confidential information memorandum or management presentation: extracts the financials into a usable model input, separates disclosed fact from seller framing, identifies adjustments and add-backs that need challenge, and produces a diligence question list. Use when the user asks to "tear down this CIM", "extract the financials from this deck", "what should we challenge in this memorandum", "review the management presentation", or "build diligence questions from the CIM". Do NOT use for the investment recommendation — use ic-memo instead; a multi-document data room review — use document-diligence instead; a public company profile — use company-profile-pack instead; or the returns model.
- **company-profile-pack** — Builds a sourced profile or tear sheet on a public company from SEC filings, its IR site and news: business, segment financials, recent developments, capital structure and management, every figure cited and dated. Use when the user asks to "build a company profile", "give me a one-pager on this company", "pull a tear sheet", "background on this issuer", "tell me about Acme Corp", or "what does this company actually do". Do NOT use for a single quarter's results — use earnings-note instead; peer multiples — use comps-analysis instead; acquisition accretion — use merger-model instead; slide decks — use pitch-deck-skeleton instead; meeting prep — use coverage-meeting-prep-debrief instead; or an investment recommendation — use ic-memo instead.
- **comps-analysis** — Builds a comparable company analysis: selects a defensible peer set with stated inclusion criteria, pulls trading multiples and operating metrics, calculates the median and quartiles, and applies the range to a target to produce an implied valuation. Use when the user asks to "build a comps table", "run comparable company analysis", "trading comps for this company", "what multiple do peers trade at", "who are the comps", or "value this against its peers". Do NOT use for a company profile — use company-profile-pack instead; deal accretion — use merger-model instead; or discounted cash flow, LBO, or precedent transaction analysis.
- **coverage-meeting-prep-debrief** — Prepares for a client or prospect coverage meeting from mail, calendar, files, and prior debriefs, and afterwards turns notes into a structured debrief with owned follow-ups and a draft recap email that is never sent. Use when the user asks to "prep me for my meeting with Acme", "build a briefing pack for this client", "write up my client meeting notes", "capture the follow-ups from my client call", or "draft a follow-up after the call". Do NOT use for company background with no relationship history — use company-profile-pack instead; building a deck — use pitch-deck-skeleton instead; or summarizing a meeting recording or transcript on its own — use meeting-intel instead.
- **dd-checklist** — Tracks a diligence request list through a live process: sector-tailored workstream requests, which ones have arrived versus which are still owed, aging on outstanding requests with draft chases, and a red flag register carrying severity and an honest mitigant. Use when asked to "build the diligence checklist", "what do we still need from the seller", "where are we on diligence", "track the data room", or "what is outstanding". Do NOT use for auditor evidence requests; questions from a CIM — use cim-teardown instead; the recommendation itself — use ic-memo instead; or reading the documents into an issues list — use document-diligence instead.
- **document-diligence** — Digests a set of supplied deal documents (data room files, contracts, financial statements, board decks, cap tables) into a cited summary, a cross-document discrepancy check, and a running issues list and seller Q&A tracker, with every finding cited to document and page. Use when the user asks to "summarize these data room files", "build an issues list from these documents", "extract the key contract terms", "cross-check the CIM against the financials", or "build the seller Q&A tracker". Do NOT use for a single-CIM teardown and add-back challenge — use cim-teardown instead; request status and seller chases — use dd-checklist instead; or public-company background — use company-profile-pack instead.
- **earnings-note** — Turns an earnings release, call transcript, and filing into a research note: results against expectations, what management said and how the tone shifted, guidance changes, model implications, and the thesis read-through. Use when the user asks to "write the earnings note", "summarize the quarter", "what did management say on the call", "how did they beat or miss", "draft the earnings recap", or "what changed in guidance". Do NOT use for a full company profile — use company-profile-pack instead; peer multiples — use comps-analysis instead; recapping a meeting transcript — use meeting-intel instead; or building a valuation model.
- **ic-memo** — Drafts an investment committee memo: the recommendation up front, thesis, business and market assessment, financial summary, valuation and returns, diligence findings, key risks with mitigants, and the specific approval being sought. Use when the user asks to "draft the IC memo", "write the investment committee paper", "prepare the deal memo", "build the recommendation for committee", or "write up the investment case". Do NOT use for summarizing deal documents — use document-diligence instead; peer screening — use comps-analysis instead; an earnings note — use earnings-note instead; or building the valuation or returns model.
- **merger-model** — Models what an acquisition does to pro forma EPS: offer terms and premium, sources and uses, purchase price allocation, the five-part adjustment stack, accretion or dilution by year with its attribution, and the breakeven synergy level tested as a share of the acquired company's cost base. Use when the user asks "is this deal accretive", "run the accretion dilution", "what does this do to EPS", "model the acquisition", "build the merger model", or "merger consequences for the pitch". Do NOT use for pitch book slides — use pitch-deck-skeleton instead; peer multiples — use comps-analysis instead; or sponsor returns, leverage, or intrinsic cash-flow valuation.
- **pitch-deck-skeleton** — Builds a first-draft pitch, credentials, board, or update deck skeleton on the firm's own template and prior decks: public-data pages populated and cited, reusable credentials pages identified, and every judgment page left as a labelled placeholder. Use when the user asks to "start a pitch for Acme", "create a deck skeleton for this client", "build slides from our template", "put together a credentials deck", or "draft a board presentation". Do NOT use for reviewing a CIM or management presentation — use cim-teardown instead; findings from deal documents — use document-diligence instead; meeting prep — use coverage-meeting-prep-debrief instead; peer multiples — use comps-analysis instead; the committee recommendation — use ic-memo instead; or accretion analysis — use merger-model instead.

## Install

1. Download the plugin package (the `.zip` on this page).
2. Upload it to your tenant via **M365 admin center › Manage apps › Upload custom app**, or sideload it for testing with the [Microsoft 365 Agents Toolkit CLI](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/platform/toolkit/microsoft-365-agents-toolkit-cli) (`atk install --file-path <zip> --scope Personal`).
3. Open **Cowork › Sources & Skills › Plugins** and enable it from the **Discover** section.

See [Build plugins for Copilot Cowork](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/cowork/cowork-plugin-development) for details.
