Six skills for institutional asset managers: RFP and DDQ response teams, investment writers, research analysts, product specialists, client service, and consultant relations. Draft first-pass RFP and DDQ answers from your approved SharePoint library with a citation on every answer; write named fund, portfolio, or investment-strategy commentary from approved narrative and current performance/attribution sources; compare two issuer filings' risk-factor, MD&A, and guidance language with metrics as supporting context; compare funds from public summary prospectuses; prepare named institutional investment-client mandate reviews with IPS/guidelines and relationship commitments; and refresh consultant questionnaires with a redline. Corporate operating-variance commentary, executive business updates, the manager's own board/forecast reviews, and standalone stakeholder Q&A are outside this plugin. Runs without a paid subscription or Finance-plugin dependency, using Microsoft 365 content, supplied documents, SEC EDGAR, and public web. No connectors are bundled. Customer connectors enrich sources, and templates change layout without expanding workflow ownership. Current firm performance, AUM, attribution, and fees come verbatim from a system-of-record connector or uploaded export; peer-product facts may come from authoritative public filings. Gaps stay visible. Applicable performance-presentation policy gates remain mandatory. Outputs cite every claim and carry marketing-material labelling where appropriate for compliance review.

> **Cowork plugin.** This is a Microsoft 365 Copilot **Cowork** app package (a `.zip` bundling the skills and connectors below). It installs on Cowork only.

## Skills in this plugin

- **rfp-ddq-response-draft** — Draft a new institutional RFP/DDQ from approved library answers, citing each answer and separating internal gaps. Use for "draft answers to this RFP", "populate this DDQ", "respond to this due-diligence request", "fill what we can from the library", or "identify unanswered questionnaire items". Do NOT use for recurring profiles with a prior submission to diff — use consultant-database-refresh instead; for client mandate reviews, use institutional-client-review-prep instead; for product comparisons, use peer-product-intelligence instead; for investment commentary, use investment-commentary-draft instead; for document formatting, use the built-in docx instead.
- **investment-commentary-draft** — Draft periodic investor commentary for a named fund, portfolio, or investment strategy from approved narrative and current system-of-record figures, with performance-policy gates. Use for "write our fund's quarterly commentary", "draft the strategy market review", "update the strategy letter", "describe this portfolio's attribution", or "start annual fund commentary". Do NOT use for corporate operating-variance or executive updates — use Finance variance-commentary or exec-update if available; for mandate meetings, use institutional-client-review-prep instead; for recurring questionnaires, use consultant-database-refresh instead; for fund comparisons, use peer-product-intelligence instead; for formatting, use docx instead.
- **security-research-brief** — Compare two issuer filings' risk-factor, MD&A, and guidance language using matched passages and explicit retrieval coverage; metrics support the diff only. Use for "what changed in their latest 10-K", "compare these risk factors", "how did MD&A wording shift", "build a two-filing change pack", or "diff guidance wording". Do NOT use for standalone corporate variance narrative — use Finance variance-commentary if available; for one earnings-event recap, use earnings-review if available; for fund comparisons, use peer-product-intelligence instead; for mandate reviews, use institutional-client-review-prep instead; for recurring questionnaires, use consultant-database-refresh instead; for formatting, use docx instead.
- **peer-product-intelligence** — Compare named investment funds or strategies in a sourced, share-class-specific matrix; describe differences without ranking products. Use for "how does our fund stack up", "build a competitive matrix", "benchmark this product against peers", "compare our fees to theirs", or "what differs between these funds". Do NOT use for issuer filing diffs — use security-research-brief instead; for new questionnaires, use rfp-ddq-response-draft instead; for recurring profiles, use consultant-database-refresh instead; for mandate reviews, use institutional-client-review-prep instead; for investment commentary, use investment-commentary-draft instead; for workbook formatting, use xlsx instead.
- **institutional-client-review-prep** — Prepare a named institutional investment-client mandate review from IPS/guidelines, relationship commitments, and prior materials; separate the internal brief and client deck. Use for "prep our endowment client's review", "get ready for this client's consultant", "build the mandate review", "what do we owe this plan sponsor", or "pull the client's IPS". Do NOT use for the manager's own board/operating/forecast review or standalone stakeholder Q&A — use Finance forecast-review-prep or stakeholder-qa-prep if available; for commentary, use investment-commentary-draft instead; for questionnaires, use rfp-ddq-response-draft instead; for transcript summaries, use meeting-intel if available.
- **consultant-database-refresh** — Refresh a recurring consultant questionnaire from approved content and a prior submission, showing prior versus proposed answers and aging-figure flags. Use for "refresh our eVestment profile", "update the Mercer questionnaire", "what changed since our Callan submission", "redline this manager profile", or "roll forward last year's answers". Do NOT use without a prior submission — use rfp-ddq-response-draft instead; for client mandate reviews, use institutional-client-review-prep instead; for investment commentary, use investment-commentary-draft instead; for fund comparisons, use peer-product-intelligence instead; for workbook formatting, use the built-in xlsx instead.

## Install

1. Download the plugin package (the `.zip` on this page).
2. Upload it to your tenant via **M365 admin center › Manage apps › Upload custom app**, or sideload it for testing with the [Microsoft 365 Agents Toolkit CLI](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/platform/toolkit/microsoft-365-agents-toolkit-cli) (`atk install --file-path <zip> --scope Personal`).
3. Open **Cowork › Sources & Skills › Plugins** and enable it from the **Discover** section.

See [Build plugins for Copilot Cowork](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/cowork/cowork-plugin-development) for details.
