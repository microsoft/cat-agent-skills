Helps company accounting professionals complete common tasks more efficiently and with greater confidence. Streamline workflows such as adhoc journal entries, reconciliations, reporting & forecasting, tax preparation, and month-end activities. ## Skills in this plugin

- **accounting-root-skill** — Read this BEFORE building or editing any finance or accounting deliverable. Applies the shared build contract across journal entries, reconciliations, financial reporting, presentations, budgeting and forecasting, and tax preparation. It selects the appropriate workflow and output format, preserves the requested structure, and requires source-linked calculations, control totals, documented assumptions, exception handling, and preparer/reviewer evidence. It ensures accounting deliverables are traceable, reviewable, and ready for the intended process. This is not a standalone workflow and does not replace a specialized skill. Used whenever a request creates or materially changes an accounting deliverable.
- **financial-statements** — Creates, analyzes, and ties out accounting reporting deliverables from source balances through final presentation and disclosure. Supports financial statements, trial balances, cash-flow workbooks, reporting packages, monthly close summaries, variance models, AR/AP aging, fixed-asset rollforwards, depreciation schedules, and footnote drafts. Calculates dollar and percentage variances, highlights material movements or unsupported balances, preserves drill-down to source data, and documents assumptions, adjustments, control checks, and review status. Use when preparing, analyzing, reconciling, explaining, or drafting financial statements, disclosures, reports, aging analyses, or supporting schedules.
- **financial-presentations** — Structures accounting presentations that turn detailed data into a clear, decision-ready narrative. Supports quarterly financial reviews, monthly close summaries, strategic budget reviews, AP/AR process documentation, department workload updates, reconciliation-review packs, variance and aging analyses, and audit or transaction-support decks. Builds an executive summary, logical storyline, concise commentary, useful charts or bridges, supporting appendix views, decisions required, owners, and next actions. Use for any accounting or finance slide deliverable, including board, leadership, quarterly review, planning, close-status, process, investment, audit, or team-meeting presentations.
- **reconciliation** — Reconciles balances and transactions across the general ledger, subledgers, bank or loan statements, supporting schedules, vendor records, and third-party data. Supports GL-to-subledger, bank, cash, loan, trial-balance, AP/vendor, and invoice-batch reconciliations, plus explanatory memos and supervisor review packs. Matches transactions, calculates adjusted balances, and classifies timing items, missing support, posting errors, duplicates, cutoff issues, disputes, and FX differences. Produces control totals, materiality flags, proposed corrections, owners, aging, resolution dates, and reviewer evidence. Use to tie out accounts, investigate discrepancies, or prepare close-review packages.
- **journal-entry** — Prepares posting-ready adhoc journal entries and supporting schedules with traceable calculations, balanced debits and credits, documented business purpose, and release controls. Supports recurring entries, reversals, corrections, reclassifications, allocations, close batches, accruals, deferrals, depreciation true-ups, payroll, intercompany activity, AP/AR clearing, suspense resolution, and FX revaluation. Reconciles entries to source records and control totals, flags unmapped accounts or cost centers, and produces line-level posting detail, narration, supporting evidence, adjustment rationale, and preparer/reviewer documentation. Use to calculate, draft, validate, correct, allocate, reverse, clear, or document a journal entry.
- **budget-and-forecast** — Plans budgets and forecasts from source-system actuals and departmental inputs through consolidated outlooks and leadership decisions. Supports loading actuals into planning templates, establishing baselines, mapping accounts and owners, consolidating P&L, balance-sheet, and cash-flow forecasts, validating assumptions, creating waterfall and driver analyses, allocating company targets across departments, comparing plans with targets, and preparing budget presentations. Produces refreshable models, scenarios, allocation logic, variance commentary, data-quality exceptions, tradeoffs, and required decisions. Use for annual budgets, rolling forecasts, reforecasts, consolidation, driver-based forecasting, target setting, variance analysis, or strategic planning reviews.
- **tax-preparation** — Prepares tax analyses, workpapers, compliance support, and audit-ready packages using source-linked calculations, jurisdictional rules, documented assumptions, and reviewer controls. Supports filing-readiness checklists, tax projections and estimated payments, entity allocations, tax account analysis, indirect-tax reconciliation, provision and effective-tax-rate analysis, tax-savings comparisons, compensation and Schedule C workbooks, master-data reviews, and transaction-level audit packages. It can also extract issues and due dates from tax notices, identify evidence gaps, and prepare response strategies, checklists, and issue trackers. Use for tax compliance, accounting, planning, reconciliation, filing readiness, or audit support.

## Install

1. Download the plugin package (the `.zip` on this page).
2. Upload it to your tenant via **M365 admin center › Manage apps › Upload custom app**, or sideload it for testing with the [Microsoft 365 Agents Toolkit CLI](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/platform/toolkit/microsoft-365-agents-toolkit-cli) (`atk install --file-path <zip> --scope Personal`).
3. Open **Cowork › Sources & Skills › Plugins** and enable it from the **Discover** section.

See [Build plugins for Copilot Cowork](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/cowork/cowork-plugin-development) for details.
