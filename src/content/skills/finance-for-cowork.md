Helps you run your finance calendar and the evidence work around it: close status, work-back plans, budget consolidation, variance narrative, review prep and stakeholder Q&A, executive updates, dataset analysis, audit packages, expense reconciliation, completing existing three-statement (income statement, balance sheet, cash flow) model templates, DCF valuation, and LBO returns models. Every skill shows its sources, marks what is missing instead of guessing, and never sends email or calendar invites without your confirmation.

> **Cowork plugin.** This is a Microsoft 365 Copilot **Cowork** app package (a `.zip` bundling the skills and connectors below). It installs on Cowork only.

## Skills in this plugin

- **3-statements** — Completes existing income statement, balance sheet and cash flow templates while preserving layout, grounding historical inputs and linking forecast formulas with reconciliation checks. Use for "fill in a 3-statement template", "complete an IS/BS/CF framework", "populate my integrated financial model", "link statements in this template", or "finish the forecasts in my three-statement workbook". Do NOT use for blank-slate models or general workbook edits (use xlsx), DCF valuation (use dcf-valuation), or buyout modeling (use lbo-model).
- **audit-evidence-package** — Maps auditor or reviewer requests to supporting evidence, summarizes what each document proves, and makes missing support visible before review rather than during it. Produces a request-by-request package with an evidence index and a gap list. Use when the user asks to "build the audit evidence package", "map these PBC requests to support", "what support are we missing for the auditors", "prepare for the audit walkthrough", or "pull evidence for this request list". Do NOT use for performing the audit or issuing an opinion, ledger or subledger reconciliation, or interpreting accounting standards.
- **budget-consolidation** — Runs a budget or planning cycle: tracks which cost centers have submitted, drafts chase messages for the ones outstanding, consolidates returned templates into one view, flags variance against target or prior year, and assembles a decision packet. Use when the user asks "who hasn't submitted their budget", "chase the outstanding cost centers", "consolidate the budget submissions", "roll up the plan templates", or "build the budget decision packet". Do NOT use for building one cost center's budget model (use xlsx), explaining actuals against plan (use variance-commentary), or valuation modeling.
- **close-status-digest** — Builds a situational-awareness digest for a financial close cycle by aggregating email and chat signal into what has landed, what is in flight, what is blocked, and what is at risk, then calling a top-line status of on-track, at-risk, or slipping with owners and unblock paths. Use when the user asks "where are we on close", "what's blocking month-end", "give me the close status", "are we on track for close", or "what's at risk this close". Do NOT use for preparing journal entries, accruals or reconciliations, post-close variance narrative (use variance-commentary), or ordinary non-close daily status.
- **dataset-analysis-report** — Takes messy spreadsheet or CSV files, inventories them, profiles the join keys, cleans and joins the data, analyzes it with charts where useful, and ships an answer-first report that leads with the finding and states its caveats. Use when the user asks to "analyze these spreadsheets", "join these CSV files", "what does this data show", "clean and combine these exports", or "build a report from this data". Do NOT use for building a blank workbook or tracker (use xlsx), valuation modeling (use dcf-valuation), completing a three-statement template (use 3-statements), or reporting from a published Power BI model.
- **dcf-valuation** — Builds discounted cash flow valuation models as banker-readable Excel workbooks with live formulas, a WACC x terminal-growth sensitivity grid, a reverse-DCF, and an explicit readiness gate. Use when the user asks to "build a DCF", "what is this company worth on a DCF basis", "run the WACC and terminal value", "what is the intrinsic value per share", or "what is the market implying". Do NOT use for LBO sponsor returns (use lbo-model), completing an existing three-statement template (use 3-statements), peer trading multiples, or non-valuation spreadsheets (use xlsx).
- **expense-itemization** — Reads receipts and invoices, itemizes every line charge, categorizes each against an expense policy, reconciles the itemized total back to the document total, and produces a submission-ready expense workbook with policy exceptions flagged. Use when the user asks to "itemize these receipts", "build my expense report", "categorize these invoices against policy", "does this receipt tie", or "flag policy exceptions on my expenses". Do NOT use for posting invoices to accounts payable, general ledger or bank reconciliation, or budget planning (use budget-consolidation).
- **exec-update** — Compresses content you already have into an executive-ready update: strict TL;DR / Key numbers / Risks / Ask structure, ~150 words, answer first. Use when the user asks to "turn this into an exec update", "summarize this for my VP", "make this leadership-ready", "give me a TL;DR for the CFO", or "compress this into a status note". Do NOT use for assembling an update from your own recent activity, org-wide announcements, long-form reports (use docx), or analysis that does not exist yet.
- **forecast-review-prep** — Builds a one-page pre-read for an upcoming forecast review, business review, or operating review by pulling the calendar event, recent email and chat discussion, attendee roles, and last cycle's open items into a structured brief. Use when the user asks to "prep me for the forecast review", "build the pre-read for the QBR", "brief me for the business review", "get me ready for the operating review", or "what is still open from last cycle". Do NOT use for anticipating specific questions (use stakeholder-qa-prep), building the forecast itself, or general meeting prep.
- **lbo-model** — Builds a leveraged buyout model: sources and uses, a debt schedule with cash sweep, operating projections, and returns to the sponsor as IRR and money multiple, plus sensitivity on entry multiple, exit multiple, and leverage. Use when the user asks to "build an LBO model", "what returns does this deal generate", "run the sponsor returns", "how much leverage can this support", "model the buyout", or "IRR and MOIC for this transaction". Do NOT use for intrinsic cash-flow valuation, trading comparables, or corporate budgeting and forecasting.
- **quarterly-workback** — Drafts a recurring work-back plan email and proposes its matching calendar invites for a period close or planning cycle. Finds the previous cycle's equivalent email in Sent Items, reuses its recipients, structure, and file links, collects the new cycle's dates from the user, pulls attendees from the prior occurrence of each meeting, and produces one email draft plus a proposed invite list. Calendar invites are created only after you approve them. Use when the user asks to "build the work-back plan for next quarter", "roll forward last quarter's deliverables email", "set up the close calendar for the next cycle", or "draft the quarterly deliverables email and invites". Do NOT use for close-week status (use close-status-digest), a brief for one review meeting (use forecast-review-prep), or one-off emails.
- **stakeholder-qa-prep** — Anticipates the questions senior stakeholders will ask in an upcoming review and pre-drafts suggested answers, each anchored to a specific retrieved number. Generates questions across five categories — number-driven, forward-looking, risk-driven, comparative, and operational — weighted toward the named audience, and marks unknowns rather than guessing. Use when the user asks "what will the CFO ask", "prep me for questions in the review", "anticipate board questions on these numbers", "build a Q&A prep sheet", or "what tough questions should I expect". Do NOT use for building the review pre-read (use forecast-review-prep), answering questions already received, or personnel conversations.
- **variance-commentary** — Drafts crisp variance commentary for actuals versus plan, forecast, prior year, or prior quarter. Captures metric, segment, period, comparison basis, magnitude, and drivers, then produces a three-part block — headline, drivers, forward look — for each segment in scope, and searches recent email and chat for grounding when drivers aren't supplied. Use when the user asks to "write the variance commentary", "explain why we missed plan", "draft the actuals vs forecast narrative", "why is revenue up versus last year", or "write commentary for the variance slides". Do NOT use for computing the variance in a model (do the spreadsheet work first), general stakeholder emails, or forecast changes not tied to actuals.

## Install

1. Download the plugin package (the `.zip` on this page).
2. Upload it to your tenant via **M365 admin center › Manage apps › Upload custom app**, or sideload it for testing with the [Microsoft 365 Agents Toolkit CLI](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/platform/toolkit/microsoft-365-agents-toolkit-cli) (`atk install --file-path <zip> --scope Personal`).
3. Open **Cowork › Sources & Skills › Plugins** and enable it from the **Discover** section.

See [Build plugins for Copilot Cowork](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/copilot/cowork/cowork-plugin-development) for details.
