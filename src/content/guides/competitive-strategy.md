# Competitive Strategy

A Microsoft-built Agent Skill for Cowork that prepares customer-specific
competitive positioning grounded in available evidence.

## When to use it

Use it for a comparison, battlecard, discovery hypotheses, or objection
responses tied to a customer's actual use case. It is not a generic product
pitch, a business-case calculator, or a replacement for a full meeting brief.

## Before you start

Identify the customer use case and competitor or comparison set. Supply the
customer requirements, approved product evidence, and relevant competitive
material, or make authorized sources available. Missing evidence should remain
visible rather than becoming an unsupported claim.

## Try it

> Prepare competitive positioning for this customer's requirements. Include
> supported differentiators, honest tradeoffs, and claims we should avoid.

## What you get

A concise summary and one editable positioning artifact with supporting
evidence, relevant objections, and gaps or questions to investigate.

## Review and control

This is an individual skill for Cowork. Check the currency and applicability
of competitive claims before using them with a customer. Preparing a strategy
does not authorize outreach or business-record changes.
