# Post-Meeting Follow-Up

A Microsoft-built Agent Skill for Cowork that captures one completed sales
meeting and prepares reviewable follow-up material.

## When to use it

Use it to summarize a completed call, capture decisions and commitments,
propose next actions, or draft a customer follow-up. For an upcoming meeting,
use Meeting Prep instead.

## Before you start

Identify the meeting or provide notes, a transcript, or other authorized
evidence. Include the intended audience and whether you need an internal
capture, a customer-only draft, or both.

## Try it

> Capture the decisions and commitments from these meeting notes, then draft
> a customer follow-up for review. Keep proposals separate from agreements.

## What you get

A concise summary and one editable meeting-capture artifact or customer-only
follow-up draft, depending on the request. Missing owners, dates, and
unconfirmed commitments remain explicit.

## Review and control

This is an individual Cowork skill. Internal strategy stays out of
customer-ready drafts. Saving a draft does not send it, update CRM records,
or turn a proposed next step into a confirmed action.
