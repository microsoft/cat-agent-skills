# Engagement Mobilization Pack

For engagement managers onboarding a new joiner to a professional services engagement.

It digests the SOW scope, separates confirmed from proposed staff, matches only released prior knowledge, applies the ethical-wall register and drafts least-privilege access requests, a joiner briefing and a first-week plan, with every decision cited.

The design bet is that a joiner should get exactly what they are cleared for on day one: nothing behind a wall, nothing unreleased and no more access than the role needs.

## What's included

1. **engagement-retrieve** digests the SOW, engagement letter, pursuit record and confirmed roster into the engagement scope.
2. **roster-pull** explains who is confirmed, who is only proposed and who is excluded or needs review.
3. **knowledge-match** matches released closeout knowledge to the engagement and explains what was excluded.
4. **wall-check** applies the ethical-wall register and builds the least-privilege access checklist for the joiner.
5. **pack-draft** drafts the mobilization pack, joiner briefing and first-week plan from the governed access checklist.

## Before you start

The SOW, engagement letter, pursuit record, confirmed roster, ethical-wall register and workspace inventory for the engagement. No connectors are required; supply the files directly or point to them in SharePoint or OneDrive.

It works best alongside two companion plugins:

- **Staffing & Bench Match** supplies the proposed staff, which this plugin keeps separate from the confirmed roster.
- **Closeout Knowledge Harvest** supplies the released prior knowledge that this plugin matches to the new engagement.

## Try it

- **Mobilize an engagement:** "Build an onboarding pack for [joiner] on the [client] [engagement] engagement. The SOW and roster are in [folder]."
- **Check walls and access:** "Is [joiner] restricted on [client]? Build the access checklist for their role."
- **Find prior knowledge:** "What prior knowledge can the joiner use on [engagement], and which closeout assets were excluded and why?"

## Limitations

Draft mobilization packs for human review. It never provisions access, grants a wall exception, assigns staff, sends messages or approves anything.

Outputs are drafts that support, not replace, professional judgement. Use synthetic or anonymised engagement data when trying it out.
