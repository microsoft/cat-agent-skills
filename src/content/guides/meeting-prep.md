# Meeting Prep

A Microsoft-built Agent Skill for Cowork that prepares a seller for one
upcoming customer conversation.

## When to use it

Use it before a customer call, buyer demo, renewal conversation, or opportunity
meeting. It focuses on what to learn, say, prove, and ask, with an
evidence-backed next move. It is not a past-meeting recap, account plan,
pipeline audit, or outreach tool.

## Before you start

Identify the upcoming meeting, customer, purpose, and known participants.
Provide relevant notes, requirements, prior interactions, or authorized
sources. Add the meeting objective and any materials you want incorporated.

## Try it

> Prepare me for this customer meeting using the attached notes. Include
> buyer priorities, questions to ask, talking points, and evidence gaps.

## What you get

A concise summary and one complete editable internal preparation brief.
Useful talking points, pitch outlines, demo guidance, and research are kept
in that brief rather than scattered across extra files.

## Review and control

This is an individual Cowork skill. Confirmed participants, expected
participants, and suggested additions remain distinct. Internal seller
strategy is separate from customer-ready language. Preparation does not
authorize invitations, outreach, or record changes.
