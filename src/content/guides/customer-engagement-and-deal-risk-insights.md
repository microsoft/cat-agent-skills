# Customer Engagement and Deal Risk Insights

A Microsoft-built Agent Skill for Cowork that explains customer engagement,
deal momentum, blockers, and what a seller can do next.

## When to use it

Use it to understand what changed with one customer or across a deal portfolio:
why progress slowed, who owns a blocker, and which next actions are supported
by the evidence.

## Before you start

Specify the customer or deal scope and relevant time period. Provide notes,
messages, meeting evidence, or authorized connected sources. Live CRM can
provide official deal facts; without it, the result is bounded engagement and
momentum insight rather than authoritative pipeline reporting.

## Try it

> Review these customer interactions and explain what changed in deal
> momentum, who owns each blocker, and what I can do next.

## What you get

A concise summary and one editable artifact explaining momentum, evidence,
blocker ownership, confidence, and proposed seller actions.

## Limitations

This is an individual Cowork skill. It does not predict win or close
probability, infer risk from low activity alone, or invent CRM facts.
Recommendations are not completed actions; external changes need separate
authorization.
