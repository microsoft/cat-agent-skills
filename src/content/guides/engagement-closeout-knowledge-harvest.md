# Closeout Knowledge Harvest

For engagement managers and knowledge management teams closing out a professional services engagement.

It assembles the final deliverables, decision log, retrospective notes and confidentiality terms, scores candidate reusable assets, clusters lessons learned, runs a confidentiality gate and drafts a sanitised knowledge record for practice review.

The design bet is that reuse is only worth having if it is safe: every asset and lesson starts on hold, and only what clears the confidentiality gate becomes a release candidate for a human to approve.

## What's included

1. **artifact-assemble** gathers the deliverable register, decision log, retrospective notes and confidentiality terms into a cited closeout record.
2. **asset-identify** scores candidate reusable assets and checks that the final deliverable package is complete.
3. **lesson-cluster** groups cited decisions and retrospective input into ranked lesson themes.
4. **sanitize-check** screens every asset and lesson for client identifiers, personal data, commercial figures, licensed material and aggregation risk, and holds anything that fails.
5. **knowledge-record** drafts the practice-review knowledge record, lesson themes, sanitisation summary and credential handoff from the governed output.

## Before you start

The deliverable register, decision log, retrospective notes and the client's confidentiality terms for the engagement. No connectors are required; supply the files directly or point to them in SharePoint or OneDrive.

The confidentiality and sanitisation rules bundled in each skill's `references/` folder are illustrative summaries. Replace them with your firm's controlled policy and client terms before relying on the output.

The released knowledge this plugin produces can feed the **Engagement Mobilization Pack** plugin on the next engagement.

## Try it

- **Close out an engagement:** "Close out the [client] [engagement] engagement using the deliverables, decision log and retrospective in [folder]."
- **Find reusable assets:** "What can we reuse from the [engagement] engagement, and is the final deliverable package complete?"
- **Check what is safe to share:** "Run the confidentiality gate on the [engagement] lessons and assets and tell me what is held and why."

## Limitations

Draft knowledge records for practice review. It never publishes to a knowledge base, clears a confidentiality restriction, approves external use or waives a client term.

Outputs are drafts that support, not replace, professional judgement. Use synthetic or anonymised engagement data when trying it out.
