# Stakeholder & Decision Map

A Microsoft-built Agent Skill for Cowork that maps a deal's buying group,
engagement, decision process, and gaps.

## When to use it

Use it to understand who is involved, who may be missing, which roles are
supported by evidence, and how the customer is likely to make a decision.
It is not a contact-sequencing or automated outreach tool.

## Before you start

Identify the deal or customer and provide interactions, meeting notes, or
authorized connected sources. Include any known decision milestones and
stakeholder information. Unknown authority or engagement should not be
filled in from a job title alone.

## Try it

> Map the buying group from these customer interactions. Separate confirmed
> roles from inferred roles and highlight decision-coverage gaps.

## What you get

A concise summary and one editable artifact containing the stakeholder map,
decision process, evidence gaps, and proposed next moves.

## Review and control

This is an individual Cowork skill. Inferred roles and engagement need
review. Recommendations do not authorize contacting people, scheduling
meetings, or changing records.
