# Work IQ sphere slide: style and layout reference

The slide is generated by `scripts/fill_slide.py` from the user's config, using `references/Work IQ Sphere Slide Reference.pptx` as the template. Never hand-type slide text: every word and number comes from the config, and the script refuses to save a slide with anything else on it. This file documents the design so it can be kept consistent.

Goal: one 16:9 slide that looks unmistakably Microsoft corporate (like the official "Work IQ: Workplace intelligence for Copilot and agents" slide), built from native editable shapes, never images or chart objects.

## Look
- White canvas with a soft light-blue horizontal band behind the middle row (E6F4FD to CCE9FB to E6F4FD). Generous margins (~0.5 in), clear grid.
- Segoe UI only. Title Semibold 28 to 32pt #242424. Section headings Semibold 16 to 20pt. Body Regular 11 to 13pt #424242 / #605E5C. Big numbers Semibold in #0078D4. Eyebrows 10pt letter-spaced #0078D4. Minimum 9pt.
- Palette: Microsoft blue 0078D4, dark blue 243A5E / 005A9E, sparing cyan 00BCF2. Entity classes: Customers 0078D4, Products 00BCF2, Programmes 008575, Themes 8661C5, Escalations D13438 (small diamonds), Assets and agents C19C00 (rounded squares), People 5C2D91. No neon, no glows, no dark backgrounds.

## Layout (left to right, then bottom)
1. Title "My Work IQ: <period> of my work, understood" (for example "a week", "a month" or "three months", from the headline) and a small subtitle with the exact date window. A title or subtitle too long for one line (after one size step) is cut at a clause boundary, never with "..." mid-phrase unless no clause boundary works; the build warns, and `text.slideTitle` / `text.slideSubtitle` set the exact words.
2. LEFT card "Business data": emails (junk and duplicate copies removed), calendar events, and Teams chats (group and 1:1) as big blue numbers (26pt) each with its label underneath on one line, "= <total> signals", a light green chip "0 things tagged or uploaded" inside the card. Soft blue triple chevrons into the sphere.
   - Files (version 1.6): when the config has `signals.files` (from `count_signals.py --files`), a fourth row shows `signals.files.worked_on` with "files I worked on" and a 10pt grey "OneDrive and SharePoint" line, the four numbers drop to 24pt so the rows fit, and the total includes the files. Without `signals.files` the row is hidden and the card is the three-row card above. The script places the card's rows on every fill, so either kind of deck can be the template for the other.
3. CENTRE sphere (~45% width): white-to-light-blue stepped fill, thin light-blue ring and faint halo, pale latitude/longitude ellipses (D0E7F8, 0.75pt), white centre circle "My Work IQ" in 0078D4. 7 colour-coded clusters of small dots (one per entity, sized by importance) with a Semibold class label and count, 1 to 3 example labels (safe names, customers bold). Thin A6C8E8 connector lines. Hyperlink the sphere and centre circle to the sphere HTML: `Work%20IQ%20Sphere%20(customer-safe).html` by default, `Work%20IQ%20Sphere.html` with `--internal-names` (or `--target`).
4. "Explore in 3D" button near the sphere: white rounded rectangle, 1pt 0078D4 outline, Semibold 0078D4 text, small play glyph, same hyperlink, screen tip "Opens the interactive Work IQ sphere".
   - "Microsoft's Work IQ slide" button top right, same style, screen tip "Show Microsoft's official Work IQ slide": jumps to slide 2, a hidden slide with Microsoft's official Work IQ slide full bleed (fade transition) and a "Back to my Work IQ" button; clicking the picture also returns. Screen tips are fixed wording set by the script.
5. RIGHT card "Copilot and agents": light-grey pills with a blue dot, one per Copilot experience in the config's `portals` (default: Microsoft 365 Copilot, Copilot Studio, Custom agents and apps); then "What it produced for me" (up to 3 of the user's evidenced outputs).
6. BOTTOM three pillar cards: grey outlined icon box with a simple line glyph, blue Semibold heading (Context, Memory, Skills), grey definition, one proof line from the user's data (definitions and proof lines top-aligned so the three pillars line up):
   - Context: "<entities> entities, 7 types, <relations> relationships"
   - Memory: the strongest trend callout
   - Skills: the grounding line of the user's first Skills question
7. Footer strip "Why it's different:" with blue dots: Built from work I already do; Respects my permissions and sensitivity labels; One foundation for Copilot and agents. Source line 9pt grey (data window, names anonymised, interactive 3D version available; with files it adds "and the files I worked on in OneDrive and SharePoint").
8. Speaker notes: present left to right; numbers are the presenter's own; with files, how many files and how many the presenter edited; click the sphere or Explore in 3D to open the interactive version (keep the HTML in the same folder); how the "Microsoft's Work IQ slide" button and the hidden slide work; with `--internal-names`, a reminder that the deck shows real names and stays internal.
9. File properties: author and "last modified by" (and app Company and Manager) are emptied on every fill; the Microsoft sensitivity label part (`docMetadata/LabelInfo.xml`) is kept exactly as in the template.

## Window and focus (version 1.7)
- A day-by-day window (the last 7 days) says "days" wherever the slide talks about time: the Memory definition reads "Recalls what matters across days." and callouts name a day ("peaked on Thu 15 Oct"). The month and the quarter keep the weekly wording.
- A focus (`meta.choices.focus`: one customer, or one programme or topic): the title names it through its `{token}` in the headline, the subtitle reads "<window>, focused on <focus>: <n> of my <total> signals", the focus is listed first in its class with "(focus)", the source line adds "Focus counts match names, mail domains and keywords, so they show direction, not a census." (names and keywords for a programme or topic) and the speaker notes give its emails, calendar events, chats and files. Customer-safe slides show the focus by its alias, like every customer. Without a focus the slide is exactly as before.

## Optional internal slide (only on request)
Same style; amber-on-pale-yellow tag "INTERNAL: do not show customers" top right; KPI tiles (white, E1DFDD border, 0078D4 numbers); horizontal bars from rectangles (0078D4 = live Work IQ threads, C7E0F4 = whitespace); callout cards with 3pt 0078D4 left bar; sources footnote. Remind the user to hide or remove it before external use.

## Accuracy checks before delivering
- Every number on the slide must match the sphere config (entities, relationships, escalations, signals). Cluster counts are complete or say "N of M shown".
- Total signals = emails + calendar events + Teams chats, plus files worked on only when `signals.files` exists. Say "edited" only for files the person edited.
- Trend wording names its period ("rose about 4x in September").
- The footnote states the data window and what was removed (junk, duplicate copies) and that names are anonymised.
